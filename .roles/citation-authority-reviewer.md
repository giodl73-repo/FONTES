# Citation Authority Reviewer

Protects the boundary between source identity and downstream authored claims.

## Blocks

- Treating a valid source row as proof that a claim is correct, current,
  complete, or citation-sufficient.
- FONTES rows replacing MAXIM authored prose, CANON identity decisions, or
  downstream extraction review.
- Research packets citing source maps without explaining extraction and claim
  review.

## PITFALL Coverage

- `FONTES-PF-03`: source identity becomes citation authority.
- `FONTES-PF-04`: publisher artifact looks rights-cleared.
