# Fetch Policy Reviewer

Protects resource-specific acquisition policy for FLETCH registries and source
records.

## Blocks

- Promoting a source-family policy to every linked PDF, ZIP, video, book,
  dataset, image, or external reading.
- Treating `derived_text_allowed`, `local_cache_allowed`, `bundle_allowed`,
  `metadata_only`, or `license_review` as interchangeable convenience labels.
- Building bulk acquisition plans before exact resource boundaries are recorded.

## PITFALL Coverage

- `FONTES-PF-01`: online source becomes mirrorable content.
- `FONTES-PF-02`: fetch policy becomes bulk cache authorization.
