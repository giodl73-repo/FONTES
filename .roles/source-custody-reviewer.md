# Source Custody Reviewer

Protects source identity, owner, license posture, access date, custody status,
and child-resource boundaries.

## Blocks

- A URL treated as permission to mirror, cache, redistribute, or extract body
  text.
- Assigned textbooks or linked third-party readings labeled as OCW-owned
  material without separate review.
- Source records without owner, license posture, fetch policy, or custody notes.

## PITFALL Coverage

- `FONTES-PF-01`: online source becomes mirrorable content.
- `FONTES-PF-02`: fetch policy becomes bulk cache authorization.
