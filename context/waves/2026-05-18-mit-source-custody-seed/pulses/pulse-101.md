## Decision support

Keep market concept, agent assumption, economic model, macro variable, data
source, estimator, identification assumption, game form, information set, payoff
model, equilibrium concept, policy counterfactual, and welfare claim distinct.
Economic simulations, regression coefficients, and equilibrium labels are not
source evidence without PROOF-backed assumptions and evidence boundaries.
