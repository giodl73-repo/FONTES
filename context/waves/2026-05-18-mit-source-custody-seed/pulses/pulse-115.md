## Boundary notes

MIT OCW course/listing/syllabus pages are mapped as derived text. Non-OCW MIT
public course/department pages for 2.810 are mapped as metadata-only. Textbook
bodies, INCOSE materials, TRIZ books, Harvard Business School cases, technical
papers, lecture notes, project files, project presentations, product concepts,
prototypes, team reports, design notebooks, manufacturing lab/shop materials,
fabrication instructions, machine settings, student products, site-visit
materials, guest-lecture materials, open textbook bodies, images, videos,
datasets, software, and linked third-party assets remain boundary-checked.

claims, and project-management claims distinct by artifact, lifecycle stage,
stakeholder, cost/schedule assumptions, risk model, prototype status, lab/shop
