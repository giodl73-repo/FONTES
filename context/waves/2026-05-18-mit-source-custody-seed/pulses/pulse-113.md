## Boundary notes

MIT OCW course/listing/syllabus pages are mapped as derived text. Textbook
bodies, lecture notes, problem sets, solutions, exams, quizzes, lab handouts,
experimental data, take-home lab reports, student portfolios, student designs,
LTA vehicle designs, project kits, OpenWetWare wiki pages, MITWorld videos, OCW
videos, podcasts, tutorial videos, simulation videos, handouts, images,
diagrams, software, datasets, readings, journal articles, and linked third-party
assets remain boundary-checked.

## Decision support

Keep structural mechanics, environmental transport, aerospace design,
nuclear/radiation, and biological engineering claims distinct by source family,
assumptions, units, model scope, design artifact, lab context, and safety/ethics
boundary. Solved problems, student portfolios, vehicle designs, laboratory data,
biological constructs, radiation measurements, and simulations are not reusable
engineering facts without PROOF-backed context and rights review.
