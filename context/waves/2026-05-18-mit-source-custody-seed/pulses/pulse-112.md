## Boundary notes

MIT OCW course/listing/syllabus pages are mapped as derived text. Textbook
bodies, OCW Scholar lecture videos, help-session videos, tutorial videos,
competition videos, lecture notes, course notes, problem sets, solutions, exams,
quizzes, lab handouts, student robot designs, notebooks, design-review reports,
CAD/solid models, software/toolbox docs, simulation models, datasets, diagrams,
images, manuscripts, case studies, and linked third-party assets remain
boundary-checked.

## Decision support

Keep design artifact, machine element, manufacturing process, experiment,
thermal/fluid model, reaction network, reactor type, transport mechanism,
material structure, bonding model, thermodynamic claim, phase behavior, lab
result, and evidence type distinct. Solved problems, robot designs, simulations,
and textbook examples are not engineering facts without PROOF-backed assumptions,
units, geometry, conditions, and rights boundaries.
