## Boundary notes

MIT OCW course/listing pages are mapped as derived text. External course sites,
Szeliski, CS231n, OpenCV, ROS 2, MoveIt, and Drake identities are metadata-only
or license-review pending exact reuse review. OCW PDFs, lecture notes, slides,
recordings, problem sets, exams, projects, student work, code, notebooks, demos,
videos, images, diagrams, screenshots, datasets, benchmarks, model weights,
simulation assets, robot descriptions, meshes, generated docs, package
artifacts, repositories, book bodies, textbook PDFs, and linked assets remain
boundary-checked.

## Decision support

Keep visual source evidence, datasets, model checkpoints, simulator state,
robot hardware/environment, and evaluation metrics distinct. Vision or robotics
capability claims need PROOF-backed source, data, model, simulator, and
environment provenance rather than demo output alone.
