## Boundary notes

MIT OCW and Open Yale course/listing/syllabus pages are mapped as derived text.
Textbook bodies, anthologies, course packets, poems, novels, literary works,
film clips, films, television episodes, screenings, lecture videos, audio,
transcripts, slides, archival materials, student papers, exams, images, media
assets, publisher pages, and linked third-party assets remain boundary-checked.

## Decision support

Keep work identity, medium, genre, technique, interpretive method, critical
tradition, historical context, adaptation relation, cultural artifact claim, and
analysis/evidence type distinct. Course topics, assigned works, film titles, and
theory labels are not interpretive claims without PROOF-backed passage, scene,
method, and rights boundaries.
