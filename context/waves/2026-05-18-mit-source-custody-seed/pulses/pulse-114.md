## Boundary notes

MIT OCW course/listing/syllabus pages are mapped as derived text. Textbook
bodies, lecture notes, lecture videos, podcasts, problem sets, solutions, exams,
quizzes, formula sheets, diagnostic exercises, handouts, lab exercises,
measurement data, student circuits, processor designs, design assignments,
projects, code, simulations, online textbook bodies, images, diagrams, applets,
software, and linked third-party assets remain boundary-checked.

## Decision support

Keep circuit abstractions, signal transforms, digital-system structures, device
models, and electromagnetic-field claims distinct by source family, units,
assumptions, lab context, model scope, and design artifact. Solved circuits,
processor labs, waveform examples, device calculations, EM derivations, and
textbook examples are not reusable engineering facts without PROOF-backed
context and rights review.
