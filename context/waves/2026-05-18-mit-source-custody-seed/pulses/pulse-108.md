## Decision support

Keep event, period, actor, institution, primary source, secondary
interpretation, method, geography, chronology, causal claim, and memory or
cultural representation distinct. Course topics, lecture titles, and textbook
assignments are not historical facts without PROOF-backed source context and
evidence type.
