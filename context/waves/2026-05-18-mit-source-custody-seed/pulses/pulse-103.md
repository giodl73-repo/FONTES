## Boundary notes

MIT OCW course/listing/syllabus/collection pages are mapped as derived text.
Problem sets, exams, readings, textbooks, case studies, team projects,
spreadsheets, real-data files, walking-tour materials, public-meeting artifacts,
lecture notes, examples, PDFs, images, diagrams, and linked third-party materials
remain boundary-checked.

## Decision support

Keep demand model, route/network, mode, cost model, operating constraint,
pricing rule, vehicle/fleet state, inventory assumption, infrastructure finance,
project evaluation, policy objective, and equity/environment claim distinct.
Route plans, logistics optimizations, or project scores are not source evidence
without PROOF-backed data, models, scenarios, constraints, and uncertainty
boundaries.
