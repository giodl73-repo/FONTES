## Boundary notes

MIT OCW course/listing pages are mapped as derived text. Current MIT course
sites, online books, Fowler practice pages, Google engineering practices, and
Google testing blog identities are metadata-only pending license and extraction
review. OCW PDFs, reading pages, quizzes, problem sets, assignments, solutions,
project assets, generated course pages, book chapters, web-edition material,
code, repositories, blog bodies, comments, images, diagrams, videos, and linked
assets remain boundary-checked.

## Decision support

Keep specs, invariants, tests, review, modularity, version-control hygiene,
architecture boundaries, and maintainability as explicit quality dimensions.
Do not treat passing tests as proof of design quality, security, or long-term
maintainability.
