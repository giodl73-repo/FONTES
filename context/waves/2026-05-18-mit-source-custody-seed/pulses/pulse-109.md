## Boundary notes

MIT OCW and Yale course/listing/syllabus pages are mapped as derived text.
Textbook bodies, anthologies, handouts, readings, course packets, articles,
scriptures, translations, lecture notes, videos, audio, transcripts, exams,
student papers, discussion posts, images, publisher pages, and linked
third-party assets remain boundary-checked.

## Decision support

Keep moral claim, normative theory, metaethical position, case context,
argument structure, religious text, translation or edition, interpretive
method, historical setting, and theological versus historical claim distinct.
Course topics, scriptural passages, and philosopher or theologian names are not
settled claims without PROOF-backed argument and interpretive context.
