## Boundary notes

MIT OCW course/listing/syllabus/collection pages are mapped as derived text.
Problem sets, exams, projects, textbooks, PDFs, lecture videos, OLL interactive
features, model files, datasets, student work, external program pages, news
links, images, diagrams, and linked third-party materials remain
boundary-checked.

## Decision support

Keep climate observation, physical mechanism, model hierarchy, emissions
pathway, atmospheric-chemistry process, energy technology, policy/economic goal,
mitigation/adaptation proposal, and sequestration claim distinct. Model outputs
or energy-system scores are not source evidence without source-backed inputs,
assumptions, model version, uncertainty, and system-boundary provenance.
