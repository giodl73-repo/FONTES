## Decision support

Keep psychological construct, neural substrate, learning environment,
participant/sample, task, measure, experimental design, statistical claim,
developmental context, and behavioral interpretation distinct. Course topics,
demos, UX observations, and experiment summaries are not general human-behavior
facts without PROOF-backed methods, populations, and evidence boundaries.
