# FONTES Phases

FONTES uses wave records to keep source-custody work explicit and reviewable.

## Phase 1 - Foundation

Establish the repo, product thesis, schemas, MIT seed records, and validation
contract.

## Phase 2 - MIT 18.06 Source Map

Enumerate all 18.06 OCW source surfaces, distinguish OCW materials from assigned
textbooks, and set fetch policy per resource group.

## Phase 3 - MIT AI Source Map

Register OCW AI/ML courses and MIT Press or author-hosted AI books with explicit
license posture and cache/metadata-only decisions.

## Phase 4 - Fetch and Custody Gate

Use FLETCH registries to fetch only license-cleared materials, record hashes and
freshness, and keep restricted sources metadata-only.

## Phase 5 - Publisher Layer

Generate PROOF custody records, PEBBLE packs where text transfer is permitted,
and CROP indices/status views for downstream MAXIM and CANON consumers.
