# MIT OpenCourseWare Terms

## Source

`https://ocw.mit.edu/pages/privacy-and-terms-of-use/`

Accessed: 2026-05-18

## License statement

MIT OpenCourseWare states that the notices and licenses on the terms page
comprise the MIT OpenCourseWare License. The Creative Commons section identifies
the license as **Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International
(CC BY-NC-SA 4.0)**.

The terms page says users are free to:

- share: copy and redistribute the material in any medium or format;
- adapt: remix, transform, and build upon the material.

The required conditions are attribution, noncommercial use, and share-alike
distribution of adaptations. MIT names, logos, and seals may be used only for
attribution unless MIT grants written consent.

## FONTES policy

| Material class | Fetch policy | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| OCW-owned course pages | `derived_text_allowed` | CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 allows sharing and adaptation for noncommercial use with attribution/share-alike. |
| OCW-owned downloadable materials | `bundle_allowed` | Source bytes may be copied/redistributed noncommercially under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 when the material is part of OCW and not separately restricted. |
| Assigned textbooks | `metadata_only` | Assignment by an OCW course does not make a textbook OCW-licensed. |
| Linked third-party PDFs/sites | `license_review` | Each linked external resource may have its own rights statement. |
| MIT name/logos | `metadata_only` | Use only for attribution; do not use for endorsement or branding. |

## Attribution baseline

Generated records that carry OCW-derived text should include:

- source course URL;
- "MIT OpenCourseWare" attribution;
- CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license link;
- access date;
- note that MIT does not endorse FONTES or downstream generated artifacts.
