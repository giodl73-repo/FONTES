# CMS.615 Games for Social Change Custody

## Source

MIT OpenCourseWare course page:
`https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/cms-615-games-for-social-change-fall-2013/`

## Current custody decision

CMS.615 extends the game-design support lane toward serious games, civic
engagement, social-change design, prototype reflection, impact assessment, and
the practical limits of games as intervention media.

OCW-owned course pages, syllabus text, assignment prompts, and the downloadable
course package are mapped under MIT OCW CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. The serious-games list
contains third-party titles and URLs; it is useful source-identity and
assessment-context metadata, but FONTES must not mirror game content or infer
rights. The readings page is sparse in the current capture and remains a
metadata boundary until stable reading rows with explicit terms are captured.

## Fetch policy

| Source | Policy | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Course page | `derived_text_allowed` | OCW-owned course description and resource metadata. |
| Syllabus | `derived_text_allowed` | OCW-owned course goals, topics, requirements, and grading. |
| Assignments | `derived_text_allowed` | OCW-owned prompts for game assessment, prototype reflection, alpha presentation, and final paper. |
| Serious games list | `metadata_only` | Third-party games and publisher/developer metadata require separate source records and rights review. |
| Readings page | `metadata_only` | Sparse capture and likely third-party reading list. |
| Course download | `bundle_allowed` | OCW course ZIP contains online course content. |

## Portfolio relevance

This course supports games-design and civic/educational product repos by adding
an explicit impact-oriented game-design corpus: evaluating serious games,
designing for civic engagement, reflecting on prototype experience, and
communicating limits and lessons learned from social-change games.
