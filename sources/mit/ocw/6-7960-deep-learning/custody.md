# 6.7960 Deep Learning Custody

## Source

MIT OpenCourseWare course page:
`https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-7960-deep-learning-fall-2024/`

## Current custody decision

6.7960 is a modern MIT deep-learning source map. OCW-owned course pages,
syllabus text, reading-list metadata, project instructions, and video-gallery
metadata are eligible for FONTES extraction under MIT OCW terms.

Video media is kept `local_cache_allowed`. Readings point to a mix of OCW
resources, the Vision Book under CC BY-NC-ND, arXiv papers, external pages, and
publisher links; those linked bodies are not treated as redistributable FONTES
corpus text without separate terms. Homework artifacts and project-template ZIPs
remain review-gated.

## Fetch policy

| Source | Policy | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Course, syllabus, readings page metadata, project pages | `derived_text_allowed` | OCW terms allow sharing/adaptation under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. |
| Video gallery metadata | `derived_text_allowed` | Page metadata is OCW text. |
| Video media bytes | `local_cache_allowed` | Do not bundle media without media-specific clearance. |
| Linked readings | `license_review` | External books, arXiv papers, blogs, and publisher pages have their own terms. |
| Homework/project ZIP artifacts | `license_review` | Resource packages require child-resource inventory before bundling. |
