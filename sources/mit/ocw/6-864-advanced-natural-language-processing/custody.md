# 6.864 Advanced Natural Language Processing Custody

## Source

MIT OpenCourseWare course page:
`https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-864-advanced-natural-language-processing-fall-2005/`

## Current custody decision

6.864 is a MIT AI/NLP source map. OCW-owned course pages and lecture-note PDFs
are eligible for `derived_text_allowed` handling under the MIT OCW terms recorded
in `sources\mit\ocw\TERMS.md`.

Homework pages are mapped, but datasets, corpora, tagsets, generated feature
files, compressed archives, external tools, and textbook references are
review-gated before any bundling or extracted corpus use.

## Fetch policy

| Source | Policy | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Course, syllabus, lecture notes | `derived_text_allowed` | OCW terms allow sharing/adaptation under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. |
| Homework page text | `derived_text_allowed` | OCW page metadata is cleared. |
| Homework datasets/corpora/tools | `license_review` | NLP datasets may embed third-party corpus or tool rights. |
| Suggested textbooks | `metadata_only` | Assignment by syllabus does not clear book text for caching. |
