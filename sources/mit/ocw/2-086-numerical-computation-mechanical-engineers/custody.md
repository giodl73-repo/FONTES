## Current custody decision

2.086 is a numerical simulation support source for FONTES. It anchors
programming-first numerical computation for mechanical engineering: numerical
calculus, elementary programming, Monte Carlo methods, least squares,
regression, ordinary differential equations, sparse linear systems, nonlinear
equations, MATLAB workflows, uncertainty/error reporting, and examples from
robotics, dynamics, and structural analysis.

OCW-owned course page, syllabus, calendar, readings page, OCW-hosted unit PDFs,
assignment navigation, and course download page are mapped under MIT OCW CC
BY-NC-SA 4.0. Exam shell pages, demonstration video bytes, MATLAB scripts,
student submissions, and external textbook/resource uses remain boundary
surfaces.

## Portfolio relevance

This course fills the bridge between theory-heavy numerical methods and
implementation-heavy simulation repos. It is useful for game/simulation systems
that need practical numerical programming, sparse matrix workflows, ODE solvers,
Monte Carlo uncertainty, robotics/dynamics examples, and disciplined reporting
of numerical error.
