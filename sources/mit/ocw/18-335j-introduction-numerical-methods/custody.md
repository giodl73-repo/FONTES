## Current custody decision

18.335J is an advanced numerical-methods and numerical-linear-algebra source for
FONTES. It anchors floating-point arithmetic, backward error analysis,
conditioning, stability, direct and iterative methods, QR/SVD/eigenvalue
algorithms, sparse and structured matrices, memory/cache effects, Krylov and
preconditioning methods, optimization, adjoint methods, numerical integration,
Chebyshev approximation, DFT/FFT, and FFTW.

OCW-owned course page, syllabus, resource index, representative lecture pages,
OCW-hosted handouts, problem sets, exam PDFs, solutions, and course download page
are mapped under MIT OCW CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Julia notebooks, GitHub/nbviewer
content, external texts, final project implementations, external diagrams, and
third-party resource links remain boundary surfaces.
