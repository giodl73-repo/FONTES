# CMS.608 / CMS.864 Game Design Custody

## Source

MIT OpenCourseWare course page:
`https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/cms-608-game-design-spring-2014/`

## Current custody decision

CMS.608 is the first direct game-design support source in FONTES. It covers
non-digital game analysis and design, rule writing, iterative prototyping,
playtesting, mechanics/dynamics/aesthetics, player experience, randomness,
multiplayer systems, simulation, social play, and team design practice.

OCW-owned course pages, syllabus text, instructor insights, calendar/session
metadata, assignment PDFs, rules-style guide, and the course download package
are mapped under MIT OCW CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. The assigned readings and referenced
commercial games are metadata boundaries; do not extract third-party book text,
external game rules, BoardGameGeek pages, or linked external PDFs unless a
separate source record captures explicit reuse terms.

## Portfolio relevance

This course anchors games-design reasoning directly: rule clarity, playtesting,
iteration loops, feedback response, mechanics and dynamics, game labs, player
experience, simulation gaps, team design practice, and analog-game structures
that can inform BANISH, AMAZE, MUDDLE, TIGRIS, and future game-system repos.
