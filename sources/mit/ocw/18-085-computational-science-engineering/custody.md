# 18.085 Computational Science and Engineering I Custody

## Source

MIT OpenCourseWare course page:
`https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-085-computational-science-and-engineering-i-fall-2008/`

## Current custody decision

18.085 is a numerical simulation and game-engine support source for FONTES. It
anchors applied linear algebra, differential equations, Lagrange multipliers,
equilibrium equations, Laplace's equation, potential flow, boundary-value
problems, minimum principles, calculus of variations, finite differences,
finite elements, Fourier series, discrete Fourier transforms, FFT, convolution,
filtering, and fast Poisson solvers.

OCW-owned course pages, syllabus/calendar, assignment page prose, assignment and
exam PDFs, and course download page are mapped under MIT OCW CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
MATLAB code files, video media bytes, assigned textbooks, book pages, and
external resource links remain local-cache or metadata-only boundaries.
