## Current custody decision

6.838 is a game-engine animation support source for FONTES. It anchors
keyframing, inverse kinematics, motion capture, particle systems, constraints,
optimization, rigid-body dynamics, physics approximation, flexible bodies,
cloth, interactive synthetic characters, smoke, fluids, motion editing,
higher-level behaviors, and legged-locomotion controllers.

OCW-owned course pages, syllabus, calendar, readings-list page, projects page,
and course download page are mapped under MIT OCW CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Third-party
books, papers, linked papers, and external formatting guidance remain
metadata-only boundaries unless separately sourced.
