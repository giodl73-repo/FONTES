# 6.832 Underactuated Robotics Custody

## Source

MIT OpenCourseWare course page:
`https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-832-underactuated-robotics-spring-2009/`

## Current custody decision

6.832 is a game physics, locomotion, and character-controller support source for
FONTES. It anchors nonlinear dynamics, passive robots, pendulum and cart-pole
systems, optimal control, value iteration, partial feedback linearization,
energy shaping, trajectory optimization, walking/running models, motion
planning, stochastic control, reinforcement learning, Q-learning, and
actor-critic methods.

OCW-owned course pages, syllabus, calendar, readings page, course-note chapter
PDFs, problem-set/exam/project page prose, and course download page are mapped
under MIT OCW CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. MATLAB/code files, video media bytes,
recommended textbooks, and external updated course-note links remain local-cache
or metadata-only boundaries.
