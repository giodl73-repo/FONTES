## Current custody decision

8.02 is the companion source to the existing 8.01SC mechanics spine. It anchors
electricity, magnetism, circuits, fields, induction, Maxwell equations, waves,
and electromagnetic radiation for physically grounded object, device, material,
hazard, and world-simulation reasoning.

The Spring 2007 OCW course is preferred for FONTES custody because it exposes
OCW-hosted syllabus, reading, course-note, and download surfaces under MIT OCW
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Newer 8.02 module pages in MIT Open Learning Library are useful
as source identity and topic metadata, but their material body remains a rights
boundary until explicit resource-level terms are captured.
