## Source

## Current custody decision

8.01SC is a priority physics-for-objects source. It supplies mechanics,
kinematics, forces, momentum, energy, rotational motion, angular momentum,
rolling, elasticity, fluids, gases, and reference-frame material useful for
object behavior, simulation, and physically grounded game-design systems.

The course's textbook is _Classical Mechanics: MIT 8.01 Course Notes_ by Peter
Dourmashkin, hosted directly on OCW as chapter PDFs. Treat those OCW resources
as cleared under MIT OCW CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 unless a resource-level restriction is
later found.

drag, collisions, center of mass, energy, torque, rigid bodies, rolling,
