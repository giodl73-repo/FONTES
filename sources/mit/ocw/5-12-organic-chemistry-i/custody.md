## Source

## Current custody decision

5.12 extends the chemistry-for-real-items lane beyond general chemical
principles into organic molecular structure, stereochemistry, reaction
mechanisms, substitution, elimination, aromatics, and carbonyl chemistry.

OCW-owned course pages, syllabus text, assignments, solutions, exam surfaces,
and the course download package are mapped under MIT OCW CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. The
assigned McMurry textbook is not OCW-owned; reading references are useful
metadata but the textbook body remains outside FONTES custody.

## Portfolio relevance

This course anchors organic materials and reactions for real-item plausibility:
hydrocarbons, stereochemistry, reaction pathways, functional groups, aromatic
systems, carbonyl chemistry, synthesis logic, toxicity/reactivity reasoning, and
chemical transformations useful for materials, medicines, dyes, fuels, food, and
craft processes.
