# 5.111SC Principles of Chemical Science Custody

## Source

MIT OpenCourseWare course page:
`https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/5-111sc-principles-of-chemical-science-fall-2014/`

## Current custody decision

5.111SC is the first chemistry-for-real-items anchor. It covers atomic and
molecular electronic structure, thermodynamics, acid-base and redox equilibria,
chemical kinetics, catalysis, biological/inorganic/organic molecules, polymers,
DNA, atmospheric chemistry, environmental chemistry, and electronic devices.

OCW-owned course pages, lecture notes, clicker questions, problem resources,
exam resources, and course-download packages are covered by MIT OCW
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. The assigned Atkins/Jones textbook is not OCW-owned and stays
metadata-only. External Behind the Scenes video links need separate review.
