course metadata under MIT OCW terms. The official course site, videos, slides,
and lab repository are external surfaces and are recorded as rights boundaries.

notebooks, dependencies, data, and current-year repository drift require
child-resource inventory before FONTES bundles or extracts runnable artifacts.
