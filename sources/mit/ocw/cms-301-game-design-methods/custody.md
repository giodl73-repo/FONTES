# CMS.301 Introduction to Game Design Methods Custody

## Source

MIT OpenCourseWare course page:
`https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/cms-301-introduction-to-game-design-methods-spring-2016/`

## Current custody decision

CMS.301 is a strong methods spine for the games-design lane. It provides
introductory process coverage for rapid prototyping, playtesting, design
iteration, player-centered design, crit sessions, peer feedback, design
documentation, and game design research.

OCW-owned course pages, syllabus, calendar, assignments, instructor insights,
representative assignment PDFs, design-document template, and course download
page are mapped under MIT OCW CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. The syllabus notes that
project-specific study materials such as readings, games, and other media may
be needed and are students' responsibility; those materials remain
metadata-only boundaries. Example student work is mapped only as OCW page
metadata unless the specific resource terms are separately reviewed.

## Portfolio relevance

CMS.301 gives downstream game repos a practical design-method scaffold:
progressive assignments, critique rhythm, prototype iteration, playtesting,
project documentation, and research-facing design practice.
