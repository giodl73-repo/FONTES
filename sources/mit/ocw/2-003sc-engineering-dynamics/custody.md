# 2.003SC Engineering Dynamics Custody

## Source

MIT OpenCourseWare course page:
`https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/2-003sc-engineering-dynamics-fall-2011/`

## Current custody decision

2.003SC is a game physics and simulation support source for FONTES. It anchors
kinematics, Newton's laws, moving and rotating reference frames, center of mass,
angular momentum, torque, rigid-body rotation, equations of motion, Lagrange
equations, and free/forced mechanical vibration.

OCW-owned course pages, syllabus, resource index, session pages, problem-set
PDF surfaces, recitation notes, and course download page are mapped under MIT
OCW CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Video media bytes, suggested textbooks, and third-party
illustrative images remain local-cache or metadata-only boundaries.
