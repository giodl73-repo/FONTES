## Current custody decision

18.330 is a numerical-analysis foundation source for FONTES. It anchors
efficient numerical solution of science and engineering problems across series
expansions, numerical differentiation/integration, interpolation, splines,
root finding, Newton's method, ODE initial-value methods, Fourier analysis,
sampling, spectral methods, least squares, and principal component analysis.

OCW-owned course page, syllabus, lecture notes, assignments, instructor
insights, and course download page are mapped under MIT OCW CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
Student code/programming work, exam payloads not separately mapped, and linked
prerequisite/comparison courses remain boundary surfaces.
