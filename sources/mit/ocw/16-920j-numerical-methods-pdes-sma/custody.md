## Current custody decision

16.920J / 2.097J / 6.339J / SMA 5212 is an advanced numerical PDE source for
FONTES. It anchors modern numerical techniques for linear and nonlinear
elliptic, parabolic, hyperbolic, and integral equations, including finite
difference, finite volume, finite element, boundary element, direct solver,
iterative solver, and multigrid methods.

## Portfolio relevance

This course complements 18.336 and 2.086 by adding a formal graduate-level PDE
solver treatment with boundary element and integral equation methods. It is
useful for simulation/game systems that need solver selection, conservation-law
schemes, FEM/FVM/FDM vocabulary, multigrid/iterative methods, and robust PDE
source provenance.
