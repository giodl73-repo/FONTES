## Current custody decision

6.8300 is a MIT AI/computer-vision source map. The OCW course page is cleared as
course metadata under MIT OCW terms. Most detailed course materials are linked
through an external course site, including schedule rows, recordings, slides,
problem sets, Gradescope, Piazza, Canvas, and Google Drive resources. Those
surfaces are captured as source identities and rights boundaries, not as
redistributable FONTES corpus text.
