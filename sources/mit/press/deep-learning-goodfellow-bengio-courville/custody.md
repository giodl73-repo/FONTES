## Source

Official author-hosted site: `https://www.deeplearningbook.org/`

## Current custody decision

This is an MIT Press AI book and a high-value source anchor, but it is not an
open redistribution corpus. The official site says the online version is free to
read and that PDF distribution is forbidden by contract. FONTES therefore keeps
this metadata-only until a narrower allowed-use policy is approved.
