## Rights boundary

The official site is a source identity and reading-access anchor only. FONTES
does not cache, bundle, extract, index, or redistribute the book body because the
recorded custody note says PDF distribution is forbidden by contract.

## Portfolio relevance

This work remains a high-value AI/ML source anchor for deep learning, neural
networks, optimization context, and model architecture references, but it can
only contribute metadata or gap status until a narrower allowed-use policy is
approved.
