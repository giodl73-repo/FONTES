# Open Yale Courses Terms

## Source

`https://oyc.yale.edu/terms`

Accessed: 2026-05-18

## License statement

Open Yale Courses states that most lectures and course material are licensed
under Creative Commons Attribution-Noncommercial-Share Alike 3.0. The terms also
state that third-party content is not covered unless explicitly set forth in the
applicable credits section.

## FONTES policy

| Material class | Fetch policy | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Yale-owned OYC lectures/course materials | `derived_text_allowed` | CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 allows noncommercial sharing and adaptation with attribution and share-alike. |
| OYC downloadable course materials | `bundle_allowed` | OYC terms allow downloading/copying/distribution for noncommercial use when material is covered by the course license. |
| Third-party materials in lectures/courses | `license_review` | OYC excludes third-party content unless credits explicitly clear it. |
| Yale names/logos/trademarks | `metadata_only` | Use only for attribution; do not imply endorsement. |

## Attribution baseline

Records carrying OYC-derived text should include faculty member, course title,
Yale University: Open Yale Courses, URL, access date, and Creative Commons
BY-NC-SA license attribution.
