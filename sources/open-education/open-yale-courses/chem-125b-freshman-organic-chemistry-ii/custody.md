# CHEM 125b Freshman Organic Chemistry II Custody

## Source

Open Yale Courses page: `https://oyc.yale.edu/chemistry/chem-125b`

## Current custody decision

CHEM 125b Freshman Organic Chemistry II is a chemistry-for-real-items source map for continuation of CHEM 125a treating reaction mechanisms, spectroscopy, organic synthesis, and molecules of nature. OYC-owned lectures and course materials are covered by the Open Yale Courses CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 family policy unless a lecture/course credit identifies third-party content outside that license.

Lecture transcript text and course-page metadata are `derived_text_allowed`. Lecture video/audio files should stay in local FLETCH cache rather than git. Assigned textbooks, optional textbooks, and linked on-campus resource sites are source identities or `license_review` until resource-specific terms are captured.

## Fetch policy

- Course page and syllabus: `derived_text_allowed`.
- Lecture pages and transcripts: `derived_text_allowed`.
- Session list: normalized in `sources\tables\open-yale-chem-125b-sessions.json`.
- OYC downloadable lecture media: `local_cache_allowed`.
- Exam pages: `derived_text_allowed` where listed by OYC; check resource-level credits before bundling.
- Professor/on-campus resource sites and assigned texts: `metadata_only` or `license_review` until separately cleared.
- Third-party content and textbook references: `metadata_only` or `license_review` until separately cleared.

## Portfolio relevance

CHEM 125b supplies mechanism, spectroscopy, synthesis, polymer, catalysis, carbonyl, green-chemistry, and natural-product concepts for real-item chemistry.
