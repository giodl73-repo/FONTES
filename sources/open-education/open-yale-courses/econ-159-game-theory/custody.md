# ECON 159 Game Theory Custody

## Source

Open Yale Courses page:
`https://oyc.yale.edu/economics/econ-159`

## Current custody decision

ECON 159 is a strategy and game-mechanics support source for FONTES. It covers
dominance, backward induction, Nash equilibrium, mixed strategies, evolutionary
stability, commitment, credibility, repeated games, asymmetric information,
signaling, auctions, and the winner's curse.

OYC-owned course pages, syllabus text embedded on the course page, lecture page
transcripts, exam page identities, and normalized session metadata are mapped
under the Open Yale Courses terms. Assigned textbooks remain metadata-only
boundaries. Video/audio media and any third-party images, examples, or linked
materials should stay local-cache only or metadata-only unless separately
reviewed.

## Portfolio relevance

This course supports games-design repos by providing formal strategy language
for mechanic design, opponent modeling, incentives, equilibrium, commitment,
credible threats, repeated interaction, auctions, and information asymmetry.
