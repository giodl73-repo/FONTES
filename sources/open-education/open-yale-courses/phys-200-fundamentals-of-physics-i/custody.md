# PHYS 200 Fundamentals of Physics I Custody

## Source

Open Yale Courses page: `https://oyc.yale.edu/physics/phys-200`

## Current custody decision

PHYS 200 is a physics-for-objects anchor for mechanics, relativity,
gravitation, thermodynamics, and waves. OYC-owned lectures and course materials
are covered by the Open Yale Courses CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 family policy unless a
lecture/course credit identifies third-party content outside that license.

Lecture transcript text and course-page metadata are `derived_text_allowed`.
Lecture video/audio files should stay in local FLETCH cache rather than git.
Assigned textbooks and the Yale Press book based on the lectures are source
identities only until book-specific terms are captured.

## Fetch policy

- Course page and syllabus: `derived_text_allowed`.
- Lecture pages and transcripts: `derived_text_allowed`.
- Session list: normalized in `sources\tables\open-yale-phys-200-sessions.json`.
- OYC downloadable lecture media: `local_cache_allowed`.
- Exams: `derived_text_allowed` for page identity and OYC-owned materials; check
  resource-level credits before bundling.
- Third-party content and textbook references: `metadata_only` or
  `license_review` until separately cleared.

## Portfolio relevance

PHYS 200 supplies core object/world behavior concepts: Newtonian mechanics,
vectors, energy, momentum, rigid-body rotation, torque, relativity, harmonic
motion, waves, fluid dynamics, thermodynamics, entropy, and heat engines.
