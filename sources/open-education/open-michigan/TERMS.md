## License statement

Open.Michigan states that it incorporates a variety of Creative Commons licenses
and that the specific license is normally stated on the main project/course page
or next to/within the work. Unless otherwise noted, Open.Michigan website
content is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0.

The OER collection policy says U-M Open Educational Resources are teaching and
learning materials produced by U-M faculty, students, and staff that are licensed
under a Creative Commons open license allowing redistribution, copying, and
adaptation. It also says referenced content added after 2016-10-24 must be under
an open license that permits reuse, while older referenced content may not be.

## FONTES policy

| Material class | Fetch policy | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Open.Michigan site content unless otherwise noted | `derived_text_allowed` | Default CC BY 3.0 allows copying, distribution, display, performance, and derivatives with attribution. |
| U-M OER collection items with explicit CC license | `bundle_allowed` | OER collection policy requires open licenses permitting redistribution, copying, and adaptation. |
| Individual resources with specific CC license | `bundle_allowed` | Promote after capturing the item-level license and attribution text. |
| Older referenced content or external links | `license_review` | Open.Michigan warns pre-2016 referenced content may not permit duplication, modification, or redistribution. |
| U-M name/logos | `metadata_only` | Use only for attribution; do not imply endorsement. |

## Attribution baseline

Use Open.Michigan's recommended structure: author(s), course title if
applicable, term if applicable, Open.Michigan / Regents of the University of
Michigan, URL, access date, and the specific Creative Commons license type.
