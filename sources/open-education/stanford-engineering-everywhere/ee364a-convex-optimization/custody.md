## Current custody decision

EE364A is the optimization spine for ML, planning, routing, allocation, control,
statistics, signal processing, circuit design, and evidence scoring. SEE-owned
course materials are handled under the Stanford SEE CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 family
policy unless a file-level exception appears.

The assigned textbook _Convex Optimization_ by Boyd and Vandenberghe is a
separate Cambridge University Press copyrighted work according to the SEE course
page. Keep textbook content in `license_review` until a book-specific permission
record is captured.
