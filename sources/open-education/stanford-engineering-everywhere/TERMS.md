## License statement

Stanford Engineering Everywhere says that unless otherwise expressly indicated,
SEE content is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-
ShareAlike 4.0 International. It also says SEE content includes lecture videos,
slides, reading lists, handouts, homework, quizzes, examinations, and solution
sets, and that non-video course materials are available as ZIP files.

The course list says Stanford Introduction to Logic is not licensed under a
Creative Commons license and requires contacting the course team for reuse.

## FONTES policy

| Material class | Fetch policy | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| SEE course pages and metadata | `derived_text_allowed` | Covered by SEE CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 unless otherwise indicated. |
| SEE non-video course ZIPs/PDFs | `bundle_allowed` | SEE says PDF documents and course materials are downloadable under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 unless otherwise indicated. |
| SEE videos | `local_cache_allowed` | SEE permits downloading lecture videos, but FONTES should keep large video assets out of git and cache through FLETCH only. |
| Stanford Introduction to Logic | `license_review` | Explicitly excluded from the general SEE Creative Commons policy. |
| Course readers/course packs | `metadata_only` | SEE says course packs cannot be offered online because of copyright issues. |
