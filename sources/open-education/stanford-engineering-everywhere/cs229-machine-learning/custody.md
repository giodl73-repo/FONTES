# CS229 Machine Learning Custody

## Source

Stanford Engineering Everywhere course page:
`https://see.stanford.edu/Course/CS229`

## Current custody decision

CS229 is the first Stanford SEE AI/ML anchor. SEE states that, unless otherwise
indicated, SEE content is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and includes lecture videos, slides,
reading lists, handouts, homework, quizzes, examinations, and solution sets.

Non-video CS229 course materials are eligible for `bundle_allowed` handling.
Lecture videos are downloadable from SEE but should remain FLETCH cache objects,
not git content. External links from the resources panel are not automatically
covered by SEE terms.

## Fetch policy

| Source | Policy | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Course page | `derived_text_allowed` | SEE course metadata, instructor bio, resource tables, assignments, and lecture-session metadata. |
| Syllabus/info PDF | `bundle_allowed` | SEE-distributed PDF under family policy unless otherwise indicated. |
| Schedule PDF | `bundle_allowed` | SEE-distributed PDF under family policy unless otherwise indicated. |
| All course materials ZIP | `bundle_allowed` | SEE offers non-video course materials as downloadable ZIP. |
| Lecture notes and review notes | `bundle_allowed` | SEE-distributed PDFs/text files under family policy unless otherwise indicated. |
| Assignments, data, solutions | `bundle_allowed` | SEE-distributed problem sets, data ZIPs, and solution sets under family policy unless otherwise indicated. |
| Lecture videos | `local_cache_allowed` | Downloadable MP4s are allowed for local FLETCH cache, but large media bytes stay out of git. |
| Transcripts | `bundle_allowed` | SEE-distributed HTML/PDF transcript files under family policy unless otherwise indicated. |
| External resource links | `license_review` | Linked project pages, tutorials, documentation, and viewer sites need separate terms capture. |

## Portfolio relevance

CS229 anchors supervised learning, generative and discriminative models,
generalized linear models, SVMs, learning theory, regularization, clustering,
EM, factor analysis, PCA, ICA, Gaussian processes, reinforcement learning, and
adaptive/robotic control.
