# EE263 Introduction to Linear Dynamical Systems Custody

## Source

Stanford Engineering Everywhere course page:
`https://see.stanford.edu/Course/EE263`

## Current custody decision

EE263 is a priority mathematics and systems source for simulation, dynamics,
control, signal processing, communications, and state estimation. SEE-owned
course materials are handled under the Stanford SEE CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 family
policy unless a file-level exception appears.

Non-video materials are `bundle_allowed`. Lecture videos remain local FLETCH
cache objects and should not be committed to git.

## Portfolio relevance

EE263 anchors linear state-space thinking: least squares, least norm, QR,
regularization, Gauss-Newton, autonomous systems, matrix exponential, eigen
structure, Jordan form, MIMO systems, aircraft dynamics, SVD, controllability,
observability, and least-squares state estimation.
