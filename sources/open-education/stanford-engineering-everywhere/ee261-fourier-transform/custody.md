# EE261 The Fourier Transform and its Applications Custody

## Source

Stanford Engineering Everywhere course page:
`https://see.stanford.edu/Course/EE261`

## Current custody decision

EE261 is a high-value mathematics, signal-processing, and imaging source. It
connects Fourier series and transforms to distributions, convolution,
correlation, filters, sampling theory, FFTs, optics, crystallography, imaging,
and linear systems.

SEE-owned non-video materials are `bundle_allowed` under the Stanford SEE
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 family policy unless a file-level exception appears. Lecture
videos remain local FLETCH cache objects and should not be committed to git.
Assignment support files, including Matlab/text/audio/image archives, are
treated as SEE-distributed course materials but should receive child-resource
review before downstream package promotion.

## Portfolio relevance

EE261 supplies the transform and signal spine for simulation, audio, imaging,
optics, crystallography, filtering, aliasing, compression, linear-system
analysis, and evidence scoring over signals and spatial data.
