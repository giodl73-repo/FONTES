# Stanford Public Math/Optimization Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which Stanford public math, optimization, and control course-site source
identities should follow the CS/AI, systems, stats/data, and HCI/graphics
Stanford slices?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Stanford math/optimization course homepage
identities: EE364A, EE364B, EE263, EE261, EE365, MS&E 310, MS&E 311, and MS&E
318. These are `metadata_only` source-identity rows until course-specific terms
and child resources are reviewed.

Some courses overlap with existing Stanford Engineering Everywhere custody. This
slice intentionally treats current public course homepages as separate
metadata-only discovery anchors; it does not change the SEE bundle policy or
promote any current public-course child resource.

Course homepages can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF source identity. Notes,
slides, assignments, exams, projects, code, datasets, software, videos,
transcripts, readings, textbooks, linked tools, and third-party assets remain
boundary-checked and must not be cached or redistributed from this row.
