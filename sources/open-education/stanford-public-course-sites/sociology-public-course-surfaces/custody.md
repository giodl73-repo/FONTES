# Stanford Public Sociology Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which additional live Stanford public sociology course-site identities should
follow the political-science slice without duplicating SOC 1 or SOC 114?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Stanford sociology course homepage identities:
SOC 115, SOC 117, SOC 118, SOC 120, SOC 126, SOC 128, SOC 141, SOC 142,
SOC 180B, and SOC 181B. These are `metadata_only` source-identity rows until
course-specific terms and child resources are reviewed.

Course homepages can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF source identity. Readings,
syllabi, handouts, notes, slides, assignments, problem sets, exams, cases,
policy materials, datasets, code, software, media, videos, linked services, and
third-party assets remain boundary-checked and must not be cached or
redistributed from this row.
