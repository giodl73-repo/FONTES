# Stanford Public Advanced Chemistry Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which additional live Stanford public chemistry course-site identities should
follow the physical-sciences foundations slice without duplicating CHEM 31A,
CHEM 31B, CHEM 33, or CHEM 35?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Stanford advanced chemistry course homepage or
directory identities: CHEM 133, CHEM 134, CHEM 135, CHEM 151, CHEM 153,
CHEM 171, CHEM 221, and CHEM 225. These are `metadata_only` source-identity rows
until course-specific terms and child resources are reviewed.

Course homepages and public course directories can route FLETCH discovery and
PROOF source identity. Directory-listed files, notes, slides, assignments,
problem sets, exams, lab materials, data, spectra, software materials, reaction
mechanisms, readings, videos, linked services, and third-party assets remain
boundary-checked and must not be cached or redistributed from this row.
