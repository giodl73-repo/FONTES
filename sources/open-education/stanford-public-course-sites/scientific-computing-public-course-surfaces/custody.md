# Stanford Public Scientific Computing Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which Stanford public applied-math, numerical-methods, scientific-computing, and
matrix-methods course-site source identities should follow the math-foundations
Stanford slice?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Stanford scientific-computing course homepage
identities: CME 102, CME 104, CME 106/ENGR 155C, CME 108/MATH 114, CME 193,
CME 206, CME 306, and ENGR 108. These are `metadata_only` source-identity rows
until course-specific terms and child resources are reviewed.

Course homepages can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF source identity. Notes,
slides, assignments, problem sets, exams, notebooks, datasets, code, simulations,
software environments, applications, readings, videos, linked services, and
third-party assets remain boundary-checked and must not be cached or
redistributed from this row.
