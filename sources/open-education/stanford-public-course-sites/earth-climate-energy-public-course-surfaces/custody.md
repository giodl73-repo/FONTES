# Stanford Public Earth/Climate/Energy Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which Stanford public earth systems, sustainable-development data, energy,
geophysics, and energy-AI course-site source identities should follow the
business/economics Stanford slice?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Stanford earth/climate/energy course homepage
identities: EARTHSYS 10, EARTHSYS 105, CS325B/EARTHSYS 162/262, ENERGY 211,
ENERGY 221, ENERGY 281, GEOPHYS 220, and EE392B. These are `metadata_only`
source-identity rows until course-specific terms and child resources are
reviewed.

Course homepages can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF source identity. Notes,
slides, assignments, exams, projects, datasets, code, models, software, cloud
services, PDFs, readings, videos, transcripts, linked services, and third-party
assets remain boundary-checked and must not be cached or redistributed from this
row.
