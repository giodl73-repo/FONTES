# Stanford Public HCI/Graphics Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which Stanford public HCI, design, graphics, visualization, and visual-computing
course-site source identities should follow the CS/AI, systems, and stats/data
Stanford slices?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Stanford HCI/graphics course homepage identities:
CS147, CS247, CS248, CS348B, CS348C, CS448B, CS148, and CS231A. These are
`metadata_only` source-identity rows until course-specific terms and child
resources are reviewed.

Course homepages can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF source identity. Notes,
slides, assignments, exams, projects, prototypes, studio deliverables, code,
datasets, images, videos, papers, readings, linked tools, and third-party assets
remain boundary-checked and must not be cached or redistributed from this row.
