# Stanford Public Business/Economics Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which Stanford public economics, management science, probability, and decision
analysis course-site source identities should follow the bio/biomed Stanford
slice?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Stanford business/economics course homepage
identities: ECON 1, ECON 50, ECON 51, ECON 102A, ECON 102B, MS&E 120, MS&E 140,
and MS&E 152. These are `metadata_only` source-identity rows until
course-specific terms and child resources are reviewed.

Course homepages can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF source identity. Notes,
slides, assignments, exams, problem sets, cases, projects, datasets, code,
models, software, readings, videos, transcripts, linked services, and
third-party assets remain boundary-checked and must not be cached or
redistributed from this row.
