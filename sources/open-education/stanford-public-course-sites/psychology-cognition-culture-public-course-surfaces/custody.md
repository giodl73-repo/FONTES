# Stanford Public Psychology, Cognition, and Culture Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which additional live Stanford public psychology, cognition, culture, and
learning course-site identities should follow the humanities/social-sciences
slice without duplicating PSYCH 1, PSYCH 45, or PSYCH 70?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Stanford psychology/cognition/culture course
homepage identities: PSYCH 60, PSYCH 75, PSYCH 80, PSYCH 90, PSYCH 101,
PSYCH 110, PSYCH 121, and PSYCH 125/CTL 130. These are `metadata_only`
source-identity rows until course-specific terms and child resources are
reviewed.

Course homepages can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF source identity. Notes,
slides, assignments, experiments, surveys, datasets, readings, learning
materials, media, videos, linked services, and third-party assets remain
boundary-checked and must not be cached or redistributed from this row.
