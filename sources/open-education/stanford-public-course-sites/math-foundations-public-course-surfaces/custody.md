# Stanford Public Mathematics Foundations Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which Stanford public calculus, linear algebra, differential equations, algebra,
and analysis course-site source identities should follow the engineering/design
Stanford slice?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Stanford mathematics foundations course homepage
identities: MATH 19, MATH 20, MATH 21, MATH 51, MATH 53, MATH 108, MATH 113,
and MATH 172. These are `metadata_only` source-identity rows until
course-specific terms and child resources are reviewed.

Course homepages can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF source identity. Notes,
slides, assignments, problem sets, exams, simulations, data/code, textbooks,
readings, videos, linked services, and third-party assets remain
boundary-checked and must not be cached or redistributed from this row.
