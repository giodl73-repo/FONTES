# Stanford Public Arts/Languages/Media Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which Stanford public music, language, and arts/media-adjacent course-site source
identities should follow the education/design/law/policy Stanford slice?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Stanford arts/languages/media course homepage
identities: MUSIC 15N, MUSIC 21, MUSIC 220A, SPANLANG 1, FRENLANG 1, GERLANG 1,
CHINLANG 2, and JAPANLNG 3. These are `metadata_only` source-identity rows until
course-specific terms and child resources are reviewed.

Course homepages can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF source identity. Syllabi,
notes, readings, assignments, scores, listening materials, audio/video, media,
language exercises, vocabulary, grammar, character materials, code/software,
linked services, and third-party assets remain boundary-checked and must not be
cached or redistributed from this row.
