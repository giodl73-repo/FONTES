# Stanford Public Communication and Media Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which additional live Stanford public communication/media course-site identities
should follow the sociology slice without duplicating COMM 1?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Stanford communication and media course homepage
identities: COMM 106, COMM 108, COMM 166, COMM 172, and COMM 177. These are
`metadata_only` source-identity rows until course-specific terms and child
resources are reviewed.

Course homepages can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF source identity. Readings,
syllabi, handouts, notes, slides, assignments, reporting materials, projects,
datasets, code, software, media, videos, linked services, and third-party assets
remain boundary-checked and must not be cached or redistributed from this row.
