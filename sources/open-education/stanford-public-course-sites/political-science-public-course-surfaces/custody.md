# Stanford Public Political Science Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which additional live Stanford public political science course-site identities
should follow the philosophy/ethics slice without duplicating POLISCI 1?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Stanford political science course homepage and
directory identities: POLISCI 2, POLISCI 10, POLISCI 101, POLISCI 103,
POLISCI 120A, POLISCI 120B, POLISCI 120C, and POLISCI 121. These are
`metadata_only` source-identity rows until course-specific terms and child
resources are reviewed.

Course homepages and public directories can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF
source identity. Readings, syllabi, handouts, notes, slides, assignments,
problem sets, exams, policy materials, datasets, code, media, videos, linked
services, and third-party assets remain boundary-checked and must not be cached
or redistributed from this row.
