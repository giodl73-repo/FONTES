# Stanford Public AI, Language, and Learning Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which additional live Stanford public AI, language, learning, and information
retrieval course-site identities should follow the computational-methods slices
without duplicating the original CS/AI or NLP-heavy rows?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Stanford AI/language/learning course homepage
identities: CS25, CS124, CS224U, CS224W, CS230, CS276, CS329S, and CS330. These
are `metadata_only` source-identity rows until course-specific terms and child
resources are reviewed.

Course homepages can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF source identity. Slides,
notes, assignments, problem sets, exams, projects, code, datasets, notebooks,
models, infrastructure examples, readings, guest materials, videos, linked
services, and third-party assets remain boundary-checked and must not be cached
or redistributed from this row.
