should close the Stanford public-course-site sweep without duplicating HISTORY

communication rows?

## Current custody decision

course homepage identities from the final sweep: HISTORY 5N and ENGLISH 90.

primary sources, excerpts, books, articles, handouts, notes, slides,

third-party assets remain boundary-checked and must not be cached or
redistributed from this row.
