# Stanford Public Robotics/Autonomy Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which Stanford public robotics, autonomy, decision-making, and mechatronics
course-site source identities should follow the math/optimization Stanford
slice?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Stanford robotics/autonomy course homepage
identities: CS225A, CS237A, CS238, AA228, AA274A, ME218A, ME218B, and ME218C.
These are `metadata_only` source-identity rows until course-specific terms and
child resources are reviewed.

Course homepages can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF source identity. Notes,
slides, assignments, exams, projects, labs, code, datasets, media, videos,
hardware documentation, readings, linked tools, and third-party assets remain
boundary-checked and must not be cached or redistributed from this row.
