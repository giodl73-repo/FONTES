# Stanford Public Engineering/Design Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which Stanford public engineering mechanics, product realization, design, and
materials course-site source identities should follow the physical-sciences
Stanford slice?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Stanford engineering/design course homepage
identities: ENGR 14, ENGR 15, ENGR 40M, ENGR 50, ME 70, ME 80, ME 102, and
MATSCI 152. These are `metadata_only` source-identity rows until course-specific
terms and child resources are reviewed.

Course homepages can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF source identity. Notes,
slides, assignments, problem sets, exams, labs, diagrams, simulations, projects,
project artifacts, CAD/design files, hardware/software materials, code/data,
media, readings, videos, linked services, and third-party assets remain
boundary-checked and must not be cached or redistributed from this row.
