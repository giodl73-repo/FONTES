# Stanford Public EE/Signals Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which Stanford public electrical-engineering, signals, communication, and digital
systems course-site source identities should follow the theory/security Stanford
slice?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Stanford EE/signals course homepage identities:
EE102A, EE102B, EE179, EE264, EE269, EE276, EE278, and EE271. These are
`metadata_only` source-identity rows until course-specific terms and child
resources are reviewed.

Course homepages can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF source identity. Notes,
slides, assignments, exams, projects, labs, code, HDL, datasets, hardware
documentation, software, tools, readings, videos, transcripts, linked services,
and third-party assets remain boundary-checked and must not be cached or
redistributed from this row.
