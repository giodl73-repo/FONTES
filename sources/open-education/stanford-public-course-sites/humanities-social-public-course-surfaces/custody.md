# Stanford Public Humanities/Social Sciences Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which Stanford public psychology, sociology, political science, history,
philosophy, and English/humanities course-site source identities should follow
the medicine/public-health Stanford slice?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Stanford humanities/social-sciences course homepage
identities: PSYCH 1, PSYCH 45, PSYCH 70, SOC 1, SOC 114, POLISCI 1, HISTORY 202,
and PHIL 80. These are `metadata_only` source-identity rows until
course-specific terms and child resources are reviewed.

Course homepages can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF source identity. Notes,
slides, assignments, exams, projects, datasets, experiments, policy materials,
primary sources, handouts, readings, media, videos, transcripts, linked services,
and third-party assets remain boundary-checked and must not be cached or
redistributed from this row.
