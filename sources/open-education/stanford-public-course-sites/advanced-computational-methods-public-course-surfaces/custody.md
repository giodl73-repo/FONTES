# Stanford Public Advanced Computational Methods Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which additional live Stanford CME public course-site identities should follow
the scientific-computing Stanford slice without duplicating prior math,
optimization, or scientific-computing rows?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Stanford advanced computational-methods course
homepage identities: CME 100, CME 200, CME 211, CME 212, CME 241, CME 250,
CME 323, and CME 334. These are `metadata_only` source-identity rows until
course-specific terms and child resources are reviewed.

Course homepages and public course directories can route FLETCH discovery and
PROOF source identity. Notes, slides, assignments, problem sets, exams, projects,
code, notebooks, datasets, examples, software environments, directory-listed
resources, readings, videos, linked services, and third-party assets remain
boundary-checked and must not be cached or redistributed from this row.
