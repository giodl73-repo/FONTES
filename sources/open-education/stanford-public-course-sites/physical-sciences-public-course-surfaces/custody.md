# Stanford Public Physical Sciences Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which Stanford public physics and chemistry course-site source identities should
follow the arts/languages/media Stanford slice?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Stanford physical-sciences course homepage
identities: PHYSICS 21, PHYSICS 23, PHYSICS 41, PHYSICS 43, CHEM 31A, CHEM 31B,
CHEM 33, and CHEM 35. These are `metadata_only` source-identity rows until
course-specific terms and child resources are reviewed.

Course homepages can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF source identity. Notes,
slides, assignments, problem sets, exams, labs, simulations, reaction schemes,
spectra, readings, videos, linked services, and third-party assets remain
boundary-checked and must not be cached or redistributed from this row.
