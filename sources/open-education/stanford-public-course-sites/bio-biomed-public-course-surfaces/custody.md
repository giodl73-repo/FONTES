# Stanford Public Bio/Biomed Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which Stanford public computational-biology, biomedical-data, bioengineering,
genomics, and synthetic-biology course-site source identities should follow the
EE/signals Stanford slice?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Stanford bio/biomed course homepage identities:
CS279, CS273A, CS275, BIOS 221, BIOE 131, BIOE 214, BIOE 220, and GENE 211.
These are `metadata_only` source-identity rows until course-specific terms and
child resources are reviewed.

Course homepages can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF source identity. Notes,
slides, assignments, exams, projects, labs, code, datasets, clinical data,
genomics data, case materials, presentations, software, tools, readings, videos,
transcripts, linked services, and third-party assets remain boundary-checked and
must not be cached or redistributed from this row.
