# Stanford Public Systems/Theory Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which Stanford public course-site source identities should follow the first
CS/AI Stanford post-MIT slice?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Stanford systems/theory course homepage identities:
CS143, CS145, CS149, CS155, CS240, CS244, CS245, and CS140. These are
`metadata_only` source-identity rows until course-specific terms and child
resources are reviewed.

Course homepages can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF source identity. Notes,
slides, assignments, exams, projects, labs, code, datasets, papers, readings,
videos, transcripts, linked tools, and third-party assets remain boundary-checked
and must not be cached or redistributed from this row.
