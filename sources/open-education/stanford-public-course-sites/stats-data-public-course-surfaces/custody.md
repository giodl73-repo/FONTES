# Stanford Public Statistics/Data Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which Stanford public statistics/data course-site source identities should follow
the CS/AI and systems/theory Stanford slices?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Stanford statistics/data course homepage identities:
STATS 200, 202, 203, 205, 216, 315A, 305A, and 306A. These are `metadata_only`
source-identity rows until course-specific terms and child resources are
reviewed.

Course homepages can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF source identity. Notes,
slides, assignments, exams, projects, code, datasets, software environments,
readings, videos, transcripts, linked tools, and third-party assets remain
boundary-checked and must not be cached or redistributed from this row.
