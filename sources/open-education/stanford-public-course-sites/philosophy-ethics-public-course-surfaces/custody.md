# Stanford Public Philosophy and Ethics Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which additional live Stanford public philosophy course-site identities should
follow the humanities/social-sciences slice without duplicating PHIL 80?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Stanford philosophy/ethics course homepage
identities: PHIL 10, PHIL 20, PHIL 30, PHIL 50, PHIL 60, PHIL 102, PHIL 152,
and PHIL 188. These are `metadata_only` source-identity rows until
course-specific terms and child resources are reviewed.

Course homepages can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF source identity. Readings,
books, articles, excerpts, handouts, notes, slides, assignments, argument
prompts, essays, media, videos, linked services, and third-party assets remain
boundary-checked and must not be cached or redistributed from this row.
