# Stanford Public Computing Practice and Society Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which additional live Stanford public computing-practice, project, design, web,
and society course-site identities should follow the AI/language/learning slice
without duplicating prior software, HCI, AI, or robotics rows?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Stanford computing-practice and society course
homepage or directory identities: CS41, CS123, CS142, CS181/181W, CS194/194W,
CS205L, CS210, and CS377G. These are `metadata_only` source-identity rows until
course-specific terms and child resources are reviewed.

Course homepages and public course directories can route FLETCH discovery and
PROOF source identity. Directory-listed resources, notes, slides, assignments,
projects, project briefs, reports, policy materials, code, demos, data, tools,
hardware/media assets, readings, videos, linked services, and third-party assets
remain boundary-checked and must not be cached or redistributed from this row.
