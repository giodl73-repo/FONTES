# Stanford Public Theory/Security Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which Stanford public theory, logic, cryptography, and security course-site
source identities should follow the programming/software Stanford slice?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Stanford theory/security course homepage identities:
CS154, CS157, CS168, CS255, CS251, CS355, CS354, and CS261. These are
`metadata_only` source-identity rows until course-specific terms and child
resources are reviewed.

Course homepages can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF source identity. Notes,
slides, assignments, exams, projects, code, proof artifacts, datasets, software,
tools, papers, readings, videos, transcripts, linked services, and third-party
assets remain boundary-checked and must not be cached or redistributed from this
row.
