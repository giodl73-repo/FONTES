# Stanford Public Medicine/Public Health Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which Stanford public clinical research, epidemiology, biostatistics, genomics,
biomedical vision, and biomedical informatics course-site source identities
should follow the earth/climate/energy Stanford slice?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Stanford medicine/public-health course homepage
identities: HRP 223, HRP 225, HRP 226, HRP 251, HRP 259, GENE 210, BIODS 276,
and BIOMEDIN 217. These are `metadata_only` source-identity rows until
course-specific terms and child resources are reviewed.

Course homepages can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF source identity. Notes,
slides, assignments, exams, projects, datasets, code, clinical data, genomic
data, protocols, software, readings, videos, transcripts, linked services, and
third-party assets remain boundary-checked and must not be cached or
redistributed from this row.
