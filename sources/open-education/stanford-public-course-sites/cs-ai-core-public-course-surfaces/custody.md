# Stanford Public CS/AI Course Sites Custody

## Research question

What Stanford source-custody layer should FONTES add after MIT completion and
the existing Stanford Engineering Everywhere rows?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Stanford CS/AI course homepage identities beyond
SEE: CS221, CS224N, CS231N, CS246, CS161, CS144, CS228, and CS234. These are
treated as `metadata_only` source-identity rows until course-specific terms and
child resources are reviewed.

Course homepages can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF source identity. Notes,
slides, assignments, exams, projects, code, datasets, images, videos,
transcripts, readings, linked tools, and third-party assets remain
boundary-checked and must not be cached or redistributed from this row.
