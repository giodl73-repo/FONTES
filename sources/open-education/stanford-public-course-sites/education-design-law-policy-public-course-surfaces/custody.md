# Stanford Public Education/Design/Law/Policy Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which Stanford public education, design, teaching-law, ethics, public-policy, and
media/society course-site source identities should follow the humanities/social
Stanford slice?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Stanford education/design/law/policy course homepage
identities: CS182, CS293/EDUC473, ME113, ED169X/EDPS 410, ENGR110/E110,
EDUC 201, EDUC 211, and COMM 1/211. These are `metadata_only` source-identity
rows until course-specific terms and child resources are reviewed.

Course homepages can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF source identity. Syllabi,
notes, slides, readings, assignments, projects, project artifacts, datasets,
code, legal materials, PDFs, media, videos, transcripts, linked services, and
third-party assets remain boundary-checked and must not be cached or
redistributed from this row.
