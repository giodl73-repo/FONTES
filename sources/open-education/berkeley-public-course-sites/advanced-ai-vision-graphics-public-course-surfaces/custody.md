# Berkeley Public Advanced AI/Vision/Graphics Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which Berkeley public advanced AI, vision, graphics, and computational
photography course-site identities should follow the systems/security/theory
slice while preserving source-identity rights boundaries?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Berkeley course homepage identities: CS 285,
CS 280, CS 184, CS 180, and CS 194-26. These are `metadata_only`
source-identity rows until course-specific terms and child resources are
reviewed.

Course homepages can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF source identity. Notes,
slides, assignments, projects, code, models, image assets, datasets, readings,
lecture media, videos, linked services, and third-party assets remain
boundary-checked and must not be cached or redistributed from this row.
