# Berkeley Public Statistics/Probability/Data Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which Berkeley public statistics, probability, and data-methods course-site
identities should follow the advanced AI/vision/graphics slice while preserving
source-identity rights boundaries?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Berkeley course homepage identities: Prob 140,
Data 102, Data 101, Stat 133, Stat 159, and Stat 151A. These are
`metadata_only` source-identity rows until course-specific terms and child
resources are reviewed.

Course homepages can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF source identity. Notes,
slides, assignments, homework, projects, code, notebooks, datasets, textbook
links, readings, lecture media, linked services, and third-party assets remain
boundary-checked and must not be cached or redistributed from this row.
