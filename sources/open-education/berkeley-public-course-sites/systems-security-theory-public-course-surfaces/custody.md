# Berkeley Public Systems/Security/Theory Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which Berkeley public systems, security, databases, networking, theory, compilers,
and optimization course-site identities should follow the CS/data foundations
slice while preserving source-identity rights boundaries?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors public Berkeley course homepage identities: CS 162, CS 161,
CS 186, CS 168, CS 170, CS 164, and EECS 127. These are `metadata_only`
source-identity rows until course-specific terms and child resources are
reviewed.

Course homepages can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF source identity. Notes,
slides, assignments, homework, exams, projects, labs, code, datasets,
discussion materials, lecture media, linked services, and third-party assets
remain boundary-checked and must not be cached or redistributed from this row.
