# Berkeley Public CS/Data Foundations Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which Berkeley public course-site identities should launch the post-Stanford
FONTES wave with high-value CS/data foundations while preserving source-identity
rights boundaries?

## Current custody decision

This first Berkeley tranche anchors public CS/data course homepage identities:
CS 61A, CS 61B, CS 70, CS 188, CS 189, Data 8, and Data 100. These are
`metadata_only` source-identity rows until course-specific terms and child
resources are reviewed.

Course homepages can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF source identity. Notes,
slides, assignments, homework, exams, projects, labs, code, notebooks, datasets,
autograder services, textbook links, readings, lecture media, videos, linked
services, and third-party assets remain boundary-checked and must not be cached
or redistributed from this row.
