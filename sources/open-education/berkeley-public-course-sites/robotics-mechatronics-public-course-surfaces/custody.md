# Berkeley Public Robotics/Mechatronics Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which Berkeley public robotics and mechatronics course-site identities should
extend the Berkeley FONTES wave while preserving source-identity rights
boundaries?

## Current custody decision

This Berkeley tranche anchors public robotics/mechatronics course homepage
identities: CS 287, EE 125, and EE 192. These are `metadata_only`
source-identity rows until course-specific terms and child resources are
reviewed.

Course homepages can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF source identity. Notes,
slides, assignments, homework, exams, projects, labs, code, design materials,
readings, lecture media, videos, linked services, and third-party assets remain
boundary-checked and must not be cached or redistributed from this row.

The live CS 285/deep-reinforcement-learning homepages were not repeated here
because they are already covered in the Berkeley advanced AI/vision/graphics
tranche.
