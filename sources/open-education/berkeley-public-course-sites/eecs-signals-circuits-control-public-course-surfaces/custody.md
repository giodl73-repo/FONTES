# Berkeley Public EECS Signals/Circuits/Control Course Sites Custody

## Research question

Which Berkeley public EECS course-site identities should extend the Berkeley
FONTES wave from CS/data/statistics into signals, devices, DSP, probability,
control, and neural information processing while preserving source-identity
rights boundaries?

## Current custody decision

This Berkeley tranche anchors public EECS course homepage identities: EECS 16A,
EECS 16B, EE 120, EE 123, EE 126, EE 128, and EE 129. These are
`metadata_only` source-identity rows until course-specific terms and child
resources are reviewed.

Course homepages can route FLETCH discovery and PROOF source identity. Notes,
slides, assignments, homework, exams, projects, labs, code, data, datasets,
discussion materials, lecture media, videos, linked services, and third-party
assets remain boundary-checked and must not be cached or redistributed from
this row.
