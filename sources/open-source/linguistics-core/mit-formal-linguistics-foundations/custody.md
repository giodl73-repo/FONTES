# MIT Linguistics Core Custody

## Research question

What MIT Linguistics and Philosophy source custody should FONTES add so PROOF,
FLETCH, CROP, and PEBBLE can distinguish formal linguistics foundations from
the existing NLP/computational-linguistics and psycholinguistics-lab source
maps?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors formal linguistics custody in MIT OCW 24.900, 24.901,
24.902, 24.903, and 24.241. MIT OCW course/listing and syllabus pages are
mapped as `derived_text_allowed`. Textbook bodies, lecture notes, slides,
research articles, readings, listening/watching materials, problem sets,
fieldwork data, language-consultant data, student papers, polished-paper
drafts, exams, quizzes, squibs, errata PDFs, textbook previews, images, and
linked third-party assets remain boundary-checked unless separately reviewed.
