# Business, Management, Entrepreneurship, Accounting, Strategy, and Organizations Custody

## Research question

What source custody should FONTES add for business and management before PROOF,
FLETCH, CROP, and PEBBLE make stronger claims about entrepreneurship, market
analysis, accounting, service operations, organizational behavior, leadership,
managerial communication, strategy, or system dynamics?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors business/management custody in MIT Sloan OCW 15.390,
15.501, 15.301, 15.279, 15.768, and 15.871. MIT OCW course/listing/syllabus
pages are mapped as `derived_text_allowed`. Textbook bodies, case packets,
Harvard Business School cases, problem sets, solutions, exams, business plans,
student pitches, advisory-board materials, guest speaker biographical details,
market analyses, company-confidential material, discussion-board posts,
simulation models, software downloads, datasets, images, diagrams, lecture
notes, tutorial videos, projects, and linked third-party assets remain
boundary-checked unless separately reviewed.
