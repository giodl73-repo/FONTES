## Boundary notes

Textbook bodies, supplementary readings, lecture notes, problem sets, solutions,
exams, labs, field-observation materials, field trip materials, geologic maps,
datasets, images, diagrams, numerical notebooks/code, student work,
software/tools, and linked third-party assets are not cached by this tranche.
