# MIT EAPS Major Foundations Custody

## Research question

What MIT-major source custody should FONTES add after Biology so PROOF, FLETCH,
CROP, and PEBBLE can distinguish geology, atmosphere/ocean dynamics, planetary
science, geophysics, nonlinear natural systems, and geobiology claims from
generic climate, environmental planning, or civil/environmental engineering
claims?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors EAPS custody in MIT OCW 12.001, 12.003, 12.002, 12.006J,
and 12.007. MIT OCW course/listing and syllabus pages are mapped as
`derived_text_allowed`. Textbook bodies, supplementary readings, lecture notes,
problem sets, solutions, exams, labs, field-observation materials, field trip
materials, geologic maps, datasets, images, diagrams, numerical notebooks/code,
student work, software/tools, and linked third-party assets remain
boundary-checked unless separately reviewed.
