# MIT Aerospace/Aeronautics Core Custody

## Research question

What MIT Aerospace/Aeronautics source custody should FONTES add so PROOF,
FLETCH, CROP, and PEBBLE can distinguish aerospace design, unified engineering,
control, dynamics, structures, propulsion, and space-systems claims from broad
engineering rows?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors MIT Aerospace/Aeronautics custody in MIT OCW 16.00,
16.01/16.02/16.03/16.04 Unified Engineering, 16.06, 16.07, 16.20, 16.50, and
16.89J. MIT OCW course/listing and syllabus pages are mapped as
`derived_text_allowed`. Lecture notes, videos, simulations, problem sets,
solutions, exams, quizzes, textbook bodies, readings, labs, lab reports,
systems-problem materials, MATLAB/code/software, aircraft/spacecraft design
artifacts, mission designs, requirements/trade studies, team presentations,
project reports, diagrams, images, PDFs, student work, and linked third-party
assets remain boundary-checked unless separately reviewed.
