# Mechanical Engineering, Chemical Engineering, and Materials Science Custody

## Research question

What source custody should FONTES add for the core engineering departments
beyond current numerical/simulation coverage before PROOF, FLETCH, CROP, and
PEBBLE make stronger claims about mechanical design, heat/mass transfer,
chemical reactions, transport processes, solid-state chemistry, materials
structure, bonding, thermodynamics, or manufacturing?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors department-level engineering custody in MIT OCW 2.007,
2.51, 10.37, 10.302, 3.091SC, and 3.012. MIT OCW course/listing/syllabus pages
are mapped as `derived_text_allowed`. Textbook bodies, OCW Scholar lecture
videos, help-session videos, tutorial videos, competition videos, lecture
notes, course notes, problem sets, solutions, exams, quizzes, lab handouts,
student robot designs, notebooks, design-review reports, CAD/solid models,
software/toolbox docs, simulation models, datasets, diagrams, images,
manuscripts, case studies, and linked third-party assets remain boundary-checked
unless separately reviewed.

## Recommendations

| Recommendation | Owner | Validation | Non-goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adopt now | PROOF / FLETCH / CROP / PEBBLE | Separate design artifact, machine element, manufacturing process, experiment, thermal/fluid model, reaction network, reactor type, transport mechanism, material structure, bonding model, thermodynamic claim, phase behavior, lab result, and evidence type. | Do not treat a course topic, solved problem, robot design, simulation output, or textbook example as an engineering fact without source-backed assumptions, units, geometry, conditions, and rights boundary. |
| Prototype behind boundary | PROOF / FLETCH | Prototype provenance for engineering-design decisions, heat/mass transfer models, reactor calculations, transport/lab evidence, and materials-property claims. | Do not cache or redistribute textbooks, notes, videos, problem sets, solutions, exams, lab materials, student designs, notebooks, CAD models, software docs, simulations, datasets, diagrams, images, manuscripts, cases, or third-party linked assets. |
| Defer | FONTES | Promote specific problems, lab data, designs, models, CAD files, course-note sections, or video transcripts only after explicit reuse review. | Do not collapse mechanical, chemical, materials, civil, electrical, aerospace, and biological engineering into one proof category. |
