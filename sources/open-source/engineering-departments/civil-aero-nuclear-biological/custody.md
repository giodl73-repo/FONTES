# Civil and Environmental, Aerospace, Nuclear, and Biological Engineering Custody

## Research question

What source custody should FONTES add for additional MIT engineering
departments after mechanical, chemical, and materials science so PROOF, FLETCH,
CROP, and PEBBLE can distinguish civil/environmental mechanics, environmental
transport, aerospace design, nuclear/radiation engineering, and biological
engineering claims?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors additional engineering-department custody in MIT OCW 1.050,
1.061, 16.00, 22.01, and 20.020. MIT OCW course/listing/syllabus pages are
mapped as `derived_text_allowed`. Textbook bodies, lecture notes, problem sets,
solutions, exams, quizzes, lab handouts, experimental data, take-home lab
reports, student portfolios, student designs, LTA vehicle designs, project kits,
OpenWetWare wiki pages, MITWorld videos, OCW videos, podcasts, tutorial videos,
simulation videos, handouts, images, diagrams, software, datasets, readings,
journal articles, and linked third-party assets remain boundary-checked unless
separately reviewed.
