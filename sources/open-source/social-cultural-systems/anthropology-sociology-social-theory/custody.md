# Social and Cultural Systems, Anthropology, Sociology, and Social Theory Custody

## Research question

What source custody should FONTES add for social and cultural systems before
PROOF, FLETCH, CROP, and PEBBLE make stronger claims about culture, social
organization, institutions, ethnography, social theory, social justice,
political economy, or social analysis?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors social/cultural systems custody in MIT OCW 21A.00,
21A.01, 21A.859J, and Open Yale/Yale Online SOCY 151. MIT OCW and Yale
course/listing/syllabus pages are mapped as `derived_text_allowed`. Textbook
bodies, book previews, readings, films, lecture videos, audio, transcripts,
student fieldwork, response papers, exams, discussion content, media, images,
platform pages, and linked third-party assets remain boundary-checked unless
separately reviewed.

## Recommendations

| Recommendation | Owner | Validation | Non-goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adopt now | PROOF / FLETCH / CROP / PEBBLE | Separate culture concept, observed practice, institution, field site, method, theory lineage, author/text identity, social group, historical context, and interpretation. | Do not treat a course topic or theoretical reading as an observed social fact without source-backed context and method. |
| Prototype behind boundary | PROOF / FLETCH | Prototype provenance for ethnographic observation, interview/fieldwork method, social-theory argument, institutional claim, and interpretive frame. | Do not cache or redistribute books, articles, films, media, transcripts, student fieldwork, response papers, exams, or third-party linked assets. |
| Defer | FONTES | Promote readings, film materials, lecture transcripts, fieldwork artifacts, or book sections only after explicit reuse review. | Do not collapse anthropology, sociology, social theory, political economy, and cultural/media analysis into one proof category. |
