# MIT Chemical Engineering Core Custody

## Research question

What MIT Chemical Engineering source custody should FONTES add so PROOF, FLETCH,
CROP, and PEBBLE can distinguish thermodynamics, transport, separations,
reaction engineering, and process dynamics/control from chemistry, materials,
and broad engineering rows?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors MIT Chemical Engineering custody in MIT OCW 10.40, 10.302,
10.32, 10.37, and 10.450. MIT OCW course/listing and syllabus pages are mapped
as `derived_text_allowed`. Lecture notes, problem sets, solutions, exams,
quizzes, textbooks, readings, process diagrams, reactor models, separation
design examples, simulations, software/model files, datasets, figures, images,
PDFs, student work, and linked third-party assets remain boundary-checked unless
separately reviewed.
