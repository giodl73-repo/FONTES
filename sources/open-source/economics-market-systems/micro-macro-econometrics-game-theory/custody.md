# Economics, Market Systems, Econometrics, and Economic Game Theory Custody

## Research question

What source custody should FONTES add for market behavior, macroeconomic
systems, econometric evidence, and economic game theory before PROOF, FLETCH,
CROP, and PEBBLE make stronger claims about prices, welfare, incentives,
employment, inflation, regressions, policy evaluation, or strategic behavior?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors economics and market-system custody in MIT OCW 14.01SC,
14.02, 14.30, 14.32, 14.12, and 14.126. MIT OCW course/listing/syllabus pages
are mapped as `derived_text_allowed`. Textbooks, readings, lecture videos,
problem sets, exams, solution keys, statistical-software logs, datasets,
spreadsheet artifacts, and linked third-party materials remain
boundary-checked.
