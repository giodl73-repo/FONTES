# MIT Political Science Core Custody

## Research question

What MIT Political Science source custody should FONTES add so PROOF, FLETCH,
CROP, and PEBBLE can distinguish American politics, foreign-policy analysis,
and political-science research-methods claims from the existing public-policy,
planning, legal-reference, and social-theory source maps?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors political-science core custody in MIT OCW 17.20, 17.40,
and 17.801. MIT OCW course/listing and syllabus pages are mapped as
`derived_text_allowed`. Textbook bodies, scholarly books/articles, readings,
lecture notes, written assignments, student papers, quizzes, exams,
presentations, NSC-style exercises, interview guides, mock-interview materials,
online data-collection artifacts, datasets, projects, images, and linked
third-party assets remain boundary-checked unless separately reviewed.
