## Boundary notes

Textbook bodies, scholarly books/articles, readings, lecture notes, written
assignments, student papers, quizzes, exams, presentations, NSC-style exercises,
interview guides, mock-interview materials, online data-collection artifacts,
datasets, projects, images, and linked third-party assets are not cached by this
tranche.
