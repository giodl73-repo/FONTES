# MIT Physics Major Core Custody

## Research question

What MIT-major source custody should FONTES add after EAPS so PROOF, FLETCH,
CROP, and PEBBLE can distinguish waves, quantum, and statistical/thermal physics
claims from existing 8.01 classical mechanics, 8.02 electromagnetism, materials,
or EAPS coverage?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors the next MIT Physics core layer in MIT OCW 8.03SC, 8.04,
8.05, 8.06, and 8.044. MIT OCW course/listing and syllabus pages are mapped as
`derived_text_allowed`. Textbook bodies, MIT Press book bodies, lecture notes,
lecture videos, problem-solving videos, assignments, problem sets, solutions,
exams, recitation materials, instructor insights, simulations, figures, images,
derivations, notebooks/code, and linked third-party assets remain
boundary-checked unless separately reviewed.
