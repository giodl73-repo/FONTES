# MIT Sloan Completion Custody

## Research question

Which Sloan source surfaces should complete FONTES coverage beyond the first
accounting, operations, finance, optimization, and communication tranche?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors MIT Sloan completion in organizations, strategy, system
dynamics, entrepreneurship, and marketing. MIT OCW course/listing and syllabus
pages are mapped as `derived_text_allowed`. Cases, simulations, spreadsheets,
software/model files, decks, pitches, videos, exams, readings, datasets,
business plans, project files, student work, and linked third-party assets
remain boundary-checked unless separately reviewed.
