# MIT Sloan Management Core Custody

## Research question

What MIT management source custody should FONTES add so PROOF, FLETCH, CROP,
and PEBBLE can distinguish accounting, management-science optimization,
operations, finance, and professional communication claims from the existing
broad business/entrepreneurship source map?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors MIT Sloan/Management custody in MIT OCW 15.501, 15.053,
15.761, 15.401, and 15.279. MIT OCW course/listing and syllabus pages are mapped
as `derived_text_allowed`. Lecture notes, problem sets, solutions, exams,
quizzes, spreadsheets, software files, Solver models, online book bodies,
textbooks, cases, case write-ups, simulation games, guest-lecture materials,
readings, assignments, memos, cover letters, presentations, Q&A artifacts,
discussion-board posts, student work, diagrams, images, videos, instructor
insights, PDFs, and linked third-party assets remain boundary-checked unless
separately reviewed.
