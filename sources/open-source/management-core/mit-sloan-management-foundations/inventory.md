## Boundary notes

Lecture notes, problem sets, solutions, exams, quizzes, spreadsheets, software
files, Solver models, online book bodies, textbooks, cases, case write-ups,
simulation games, guest-lecture materials, readings, assignments, memos, cover
letters, presentations, Q&A artifacts, discussion-board posts, student work,
diagrams, images, videos, instructor insights, PDFs, and linked third-party
assets are not cached by this tranche.
