# MIT HST/Public Health Core Custody

## Research question

What MIT HST/Public Health source custody should FONTES add so PROOF, FLETCH,
CROP, and PEBBLE can distinguish pharmacology, quantitative genomics, global
medicine, health disparities, and biomedical evidence claims from biology,
biological engineering, and general statistics rows?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors MIT HST/Public Health custody in MIT OCW HST.151, HST.508,
and HST.934J. MIT OCW course/listing and syllabus pages are mapped as
`derived_text_allowed`. Lecture notes, readings, journal articles, clinical
materials, assignments, exams, datasets, genomic data, software, videos,
presentations, case studies, policy documents, patient/clinical examples,
figures, images, PDFs, student work, and linked third-party assets remain
boundary-checked unless separately reviewed.
