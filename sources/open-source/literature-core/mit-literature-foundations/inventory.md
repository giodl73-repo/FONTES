## Boundary notes

Literary work bodies, poems, plays, novels, translations, anthologies,
textbooks, course packets, xeroxed materials, secondary readings, screenings,
films, stage productions, videos, websites, online images, journals, essays,
papers, drafts, revisions, exams, creative projects, forum posts, performances,
student work, style guides, PDFs, images, and linked third-party assets are not
cached by this tranche.
