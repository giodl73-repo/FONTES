# MIT Women and Gender Studies Core Custody

## Research question

What MIT Women and Gender Studies source custody should FONTES add so PROOF,
FLETCH, CROP, and PEBBLE can distinguish gender-studies, sexuality,
technology, health, and science/race/sex/gender claims from adjacent STS,
public-health, media, history, and social-science source maps?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors WGS custody in MIT OCW WGS.101, WGS.110J, WGS.115,
WGS.151, and WGS.225. MIT OCW course/listing and syllabus pages are mapped as
`derived_text_allowed`. Assigned books, articles, films, readings, lecture
notes, editable files, PDFs, external public-domain copies, Google Books
previews, response papers, oral presentations, student-led discussions, essays,
drafts, final papers, projects, blog discussions, mock testimony, student work,
images, and linked third-party assets remain boundary-checked unless separately
reviewed.

## Recommendations

| Recommendation | Owner | Validation | Non-goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adopt now | PROOF / FLETCH / CROP / PEBBLE | Keep claims distinct as introductory WGS, sexual/gender identities, gender and technology, gender and health, and science/race/sex/gender. | Do not duplicate STS, public-health, media, history, or social-science source maps. |
| Prototype behind boundary | PROOF / FLETCH | Prototype evidence nodes for feminist theory, intersectionality, gender identity, gendered technology, gender health research, race/sex/gender classification, and gendered power in institutions. | Do not cache or redistribute assigned readings, films, lecture notes, PDFs, editable files, previews, essays, response papers, projects, blogs, presentations, or student work. |
| Defer | FONTES | Promote specific readings, films, examples, student work, or external copies only after source-scope and rights review. | Do not infer reuse rights from OCW listing/syllabus text alone. |
