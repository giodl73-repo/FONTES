## Boundary notes

Assigned books, articles, films, readings, lecture notes, editable files, PDFs,
external public-domain copies, Google Books previews, response papers, oral
presentations, student-led discussions, essays, drafts, final papers, projects,
blog discussions, mock testimony, student work, images, and linked third-party
assets are not cached by this tranche.
