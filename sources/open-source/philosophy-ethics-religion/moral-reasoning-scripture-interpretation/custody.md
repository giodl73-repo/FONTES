# Philosophy, Ethics, Moral Reasoning, Religion, and Scripture Interpretation Custody

## Research question

What source custody should FONTES add for philosophy, ethics, religion, and
scripture interpretation before PROOF, FLETCH, CROP, and PEBBLE make stronger
claims about moral reasoning, justice, virtue, responsibility, political
legitimacy, religious texts, historical-critical method, or theological and
scriptural interpretation?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors philosophy/ethics/religion custody in MIT OCW 24.02,
MIT OCW 24.231, Yale Online PHIL 181, Open Yale RLST 145, and Open Yale
RLST 152. MIT OCW and Yale course/listing/syllabus pages are mapped as
`derived_text_allowed`. Textbook bodies, anthologies, handouts, readings,
course packets, articles, scriptures, translations, lecture notes, videos,
audio, transcripts, exams, student papers, discussion posts, images, publisher
pages, and linked third-party assets remain boundary-checked unless separately
reviewed.
