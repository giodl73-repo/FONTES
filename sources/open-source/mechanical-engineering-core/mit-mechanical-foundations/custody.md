# MIT Mechanical Engineering Core Custody

## Research question

What MIT Mechanical Engineering source custody should FONTES add so PROOF,
FLETCH, CROP, and PEBBLE can distinguish mechanical dynamics, control, design,
robotics, fluids, heat-transfer, and machine-element claims from broad
engineering and numerical-computation rows?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors MIT Mechanical Engineering custody in MIT OCW 2.003SC,
2.004, 2.007, 2.12, 2.14, 2.25, and 2.72. MIT OCW course/listing and syllabus
pages are mapped as `derived_text_allowed`. Lecture notes, videos,
problem-solving videos, problem sets, solutions, exams, quizzes, open/commercial
textbook bodies, manuscripts, readings, labs, lab check-offs, lab reports,
design notebooks, oral reports, competition videos, robot/project artifacts,
software, embedded code, simulations, shop/fabrication materials, measurements,
experiments, CAD/models/drawings, student work, images, PDFs, and linked
third-party assets remain boundary-checked unless separately reviewed.
