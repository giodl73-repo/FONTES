## Boundary notes

Lecture notes, videos, problem-solving videos, problem sets, solutions, exams,
quizzes, open/commercial textbook bodies, manuscripts, readings, labs, lab
check-offs, lab reports, design notebooks, oral reports, competition videos,
robot/project artifacts, software, embedded code, simulations,
shop/fabrication materials, measurements, experiments, CAD/models/drawings,
student work, images, PDFs, and linked third-party assets are not cached by this
tranche.
