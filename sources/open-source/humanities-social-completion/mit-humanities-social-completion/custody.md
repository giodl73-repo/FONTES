# MIT Humanities and Social Sciences Completion Custody

## Research question

Which MIT humanities and social-science source surfaces still need dedicated
FONTES coverage after the literature, philosophy, anthropology, WGS, CMS/W,
STS, music/theater, political science, economics, and language tranches?

## Current custody decision

This completion tranche uses verified MIT OCW topic/listing surfaces for the
remaining non-duplicative humanities and social-science gaps: religion,
sociology, education/learning, area studies, theater/performance depth, and
writing subfields. MIT OCW listing text is mapped as `derived_text_allowed`.
Course pages and child resources reached from these listings remain
boundary-checked until each course is separately promoted.

Readings, books, films, scripts, performances, recordings, images, maps,
datasets, assignments, drafts, portfolios, workshop materials, classroom
artifacts, videos, PDFs, student work, and linked third-party assets remain
boundary-checked unless separately reviewed.
