# MIT Biology Major Foundations Custody

## Research question

What MIT-major source custody should FONTES add after architecture/urban
planning so PROOF, FLETCH, CROP, and PEBBLE can distinguish biology, genetics,
biochemistry, cell-biology, and molecular-biology claims from public-health,
clinical, or biological-engineering claims?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors biology/life-science custody in MIT OCW 7.012, 7.013,
7.016, 7.03, 7.05, RES.7-007 / 7.06x, and RES.7-008 / 7.28x. MIT OCW
course/listing and syllabus pages are mapped as `derived_text_allowed`.
Textbook bodies, textbook web editions, lecture notes, lecture videos, tutorial
videos, problem sets, solutions, exams, quizzes, recitation material, readings,
primary literature, biological datasets, molecular diagrams, sequence data,
software/tools, MITx platform-private content, assignments, student work, and
linked third-party assets remain boundary-checked unless separately reviewed.

## Recommendations

| Recommendation | Owner | Validation | Non-goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adopt now | PROOF / FLETCH / CROP / PEBBLE | Keep biology claims distinct by course layer: introductory biology, genetics, biochemistry, cell biology, and molecular biology. | Do not treat biomedical, clinical, synthetic-biology, or public-health claims as equivalent to biology-major foundations. |
| Prototype behind boundary | PROOF / FLETCH | Prototype evidence nodes for genes/genomes, protein synthesis, metabolism, enzymology, cell signaling, cell cycle, DNA replication, transcription, translation, and experimental design. | Do not cache or redistribute lecture notes, videos, problem sets, solutions, quizzes, exams, textbook bodies, primary literature, diagrams, datasets, sequence data, or MITx-private content. |
| Defer | FONTES | Promote specific biological mechanisms, experimental results, or disease claims only after source-scope and reuse review. | Do not infer current scientific consensus from course listing text alone. |
