## Boundary notes

Textbook bodies, textbook web editions, lecture notes, lecture videos, tutorial
videos, problem sets, solutions, exams, quizzes, recitation material, readings,
primary literature, biological datasets, molecular diagrams, sequence data,
software/tools, MITx platform-private content, assignments, student work, and
linked third-party assets are not cached by this tranche.
