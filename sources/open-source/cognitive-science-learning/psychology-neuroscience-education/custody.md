# Cognitive Science, Psychology, Neuroscience, Learning, and Human Behavior Custody

## Research question

What source custody should FONTES add for cognitive science, psychology,
neuroscience, learning science, psycholinguistics, and human behavior before
PROOF, FLETCH, CROP, and PEBBLE make stronger claims about perception, memory,
learning, development, social behavior, motivation, language processing,
education, or human factors?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors cognitive science and learning custody in MIT OCW 9.00SC,
9.01, 9.59J, MAS.714J, and Open Yale PSYC 110. MIT OCW and Open Yale
course/listing/syllabus pages are mapped as `derived_text_allowed`. Videos,
lecture notes, readings, commercial and free-online textbook bodies, exams,
solutions, discussion content, student projects, blog posts, R/Mechanical Turk
materials, experiment data, transcripts, media, images, and linked third-party
assets remain boundary-checked unless separately reviewed.

## Recommendations

| Recommendation | Owner | Validation | Non-goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adopt now | PROOF / FLETCH / CROP / PEBBLE | Separate psychological construct, neural substrate, learning environment, participant/sample, task, measure, experimental design, statistical claim, developmental context, and behavioral interpretation. | Do not treat a course topic, demo, UX observation, or experiment summary as a general human-behavior fact without source-backed method and population boundaries. |
| Prototype behind boundary | PROOF / FLETCH | Prototype cognition/learning provenance for task design, stimulus, participant population, consent/platform, measurement instrument, analysis script, statistical result, replication status, learning activity, and assessment rubric. | Do not cache or redistribute videos, notes, textbooks, exams, solutions, student work, transcripts, blog posts, platform data, experimental datasets, or media assets. |
| Defer | FONTES | Promote lecture transcripts, experiment materials, datasets, textbook sections, participant data, or learning-technology artifacts only after explicit reuse and human-subjects/privacy review. | Do not collapse neuroscience, psychology, language experiments, education design, and human-factors claims into one proof category. |
