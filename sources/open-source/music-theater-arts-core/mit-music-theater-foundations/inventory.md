## Boundary notes

Textbook bodies, CDs, audio recordings, lecture notes, lecture videos, music
audio, scores, notated examples, scripts, plays, student compositions, student
plays, written assignments, projects, performance recordings, guest
demonstrations, concert materials, workshop materials, software/circuit
artifacts, images, and linked third-party assets are not cached by this tranche.
