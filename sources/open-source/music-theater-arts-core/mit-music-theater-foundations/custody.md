# MIT Music and Theater Arts Core Custody

## Research question

What MIT Music and Theater Arts source custody should FONTES add so PROOF,
FLETCH, CROP, and PEBBLE can distinguish music history, world music,
composition, music technology, and playwriting claims from the existing broad
humanities, media, and game-design source maps?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors music and theater custody in MIT OCW 21M.011, 21M.030,
21M.065, 21M.380, and 21M.604. MIT OCW course/listing and syllabus pages are
mapped as `derived_text_allowed`. Textbook bodies, CDs, audio recordings,
lecture notes, lecture videos, music audio, scores, notated examples, scripts,
plays, student compositions, student plays, written assignments, projects,
performance recordings, guest demonstrations, concert materials, workshop
materials, software/circuit artifacts, images, and linked third-party assets
remain boundary-checked unless separately reviewed.

## Recommendations

| Recommendation | Owner | Validation | Non-goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adopt now | PROOF / FLETCH / CROP / PEBBLE | Keep claims distinct as Western music, world music/ethnomusicology, musical composition, music technology, and playwriting. | Do not duplicate broad humanities/media or game-design source maps. |
| Prototype behind boundary | PROOF / FLETCH | Prototype evidence nodes for listening analysis, musical form/genre, performance practice, ethnographic music context, notation/composition, music technology, sound production, scene structure, and dialogue. | Do not cache or redistribute audio, scores, scripts, plays, notes, videos, student work, CDs, software, circuits, images, or third-party assets. |
| Defer | FONTES | Promote specific musical excerpts, scores, scripts, or performances only after source-scope and rights review. | Do not infer performance rights or music reuse permissions from listing text alone. |
