# Engineering Practice, Systems, Product Design, Manufacturing, and Project Management Custody

## Research question

What engineering-practice source custody should FONTES add after the
department-foundation tranches so PROOF, FLETCH, CROP, and PEBBLE can separate
systems engineering, product development, manufacturing-process, and project
management claims from discipline-specific engineering facts?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors engineering-practice custody in MIT OCW ESD.33, MIT OCW
15.783J, MIT 2.810 public course/department pages, and MIT OCW 1.040. MIT OCW
course/listing/syllabus pages are mapped as `derived_text_allowed`; non-OCW MIT
public course/department pages for 2.810 are mapped as `metadata_only`. Textbook
bodies, INCOSE materials, TRIZ books, Harvard Business School cases, technical
papers, lecture notes, project files, project presentations, product concepts,
prototypes, team reports, design notebooks, manufacturing lab/shop materials,
fabrication instructions, machine settings, student products, site-visit
materials, guest-lecture materials, open textbook bodies, images, videos,
datasets, software, and linked third-party assets remain boundary-checked unless
separately reviewed.

## Recommendations

| Recommendation | Owner | Validation | Non-goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adopt now | PROOF / FLETCH / CROP / PEBBLE | Keep systems requirements, product-design decisions, manufacturing process claims, and project-management claims distinct by artifact, lifecycle stage, stakeholder, cost/schedule assumptions, risk model, prototype status, lab/shop context, and rights boundary. | Do not treat a design method, case study, student prototype, manufacturing lab result, project schedule, or cost model as a reusable engineering fact without source-backed assumptions. |
| Prototype behind boundary | PROOF / FLETCH | Prototype evidence nodes for requirements, DSM/robust-design use, product architecture, design-for-manufacturing, manufacturing process capability, quality/rate/cost/flexibility tradeoffs, project finance, risk, and project control. | Do not cache or redistribute textbooks, INCOSE materials, TRIZ books, HBS cases, technical papers, notes, presentations, prototypes, reports, lab/shop materials, machine settings, guest materials, site visit materials, videos, datasets, software, images, or linked third-party assets. |
| Defer | FONTES | Promote concrete product prototypes, process parameters, project estimates, case analyses, term-project reports, or manufacturing lab data only after explicit reuse review. | Do not collapse engineering practice into department facts or treat non-OCW MIT course pages as OCW-derived text. |
