## Boundary notes

Lecture notes, assigned readings, primary-text bodies, secondary-reading bodies,
written assignments, papers, rewrites, exercises, exams, presentations,
recitation artifacts, student work, external writing guidelines, policy pages,
images, PDFs, and linked third-party assets are not cached by this tranche.
