# MIT Philosophy Core Custody

## Research question

What MIT Philosophy source custody should FONTES add so PROOF, FLETCH, CROP,
and PEBBLE can distinguish Western-philosophy, ethics, food-politics,
philosophy-of-mind, and epistemology claims from the existing broad
philosophy/ethics/religion source map?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors MIT Philosophy custody in MIT OCW 24.01, 24.02, 24.03,
24.09, and 24.211. MIT OCW course/listing and syllabus pages are mapped as
`derived_text_allowed`. Lecture notes, assigned readings, primary-text bodies,
secondary-reading bodies, written assignments, papers, rewrites, exercises,
exams, presentations, recitation artifacts, student work, external writing
guidelines, policy pages, images, PDFs, and linked third-party assets remain
boundary-checked unless separately reviewed.
