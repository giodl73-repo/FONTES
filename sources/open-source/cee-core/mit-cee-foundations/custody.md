# MIT Civil and Environmental Engineering Core Custody

## Research question

What MIT Civil and Environmental Engineering source custody should FONTES add so
PROOF, FLETCH, CROP, and PEBBLE can distinguish CEE design, structures,
solid/fluid mechanics, environmental transport, hydrology, and geotechnical
claims from broad engineering and urban-planning rows?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors MIT Civil and Environmental Engineering custody in MIT OCW
1.101, 1.050, 1.060, 1.061, 1.72, and 1.364. MIT OCW course/listing and
syllabus pages are mapped as `derived_text_allowed`. Lecture notes, problem
sets, solutions, exams, quizzes, textbooks, readings, labs, field materials,
site data, environmental datasets, hydrologic/geotechnical models, software,
maps, diagrams, images, design projects, student portfolios, presentations,
reports, PDFs, and linked third-party assets remain boundary-checked unless
separately reviewed.
