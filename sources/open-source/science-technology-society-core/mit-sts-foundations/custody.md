# MIT Science, Technology, and Society Core Custody

## Research question

What MIT Science, Technology, and Society source custody should FONTES add so
PROOF, FLETCH, CROP, and PEBBLE can distinguish STS claims about technology,
science, medicine, ethics, politics, and environmental systems from adjacent
history, policy, public-health, climate, and media source maps?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors STS custody in MIT OCW STS.001, STS.003, STS.005, STS.011,
and STS.032. MIT OCW course/listing and syllabus pages are mapped as
`derived_text_allowed`. Assigned books, articles, films, videos, lecture notes,
readings, papers, quizzes, exams, discussion prompts, student writing, drafts,
projects, blog posts, podcasts, infographics, guest-lecture materials, textbook
bodies, Google Books previews, PDFs, images, and linked third-party assets remain
boundary-checked unless separately reviewed.
