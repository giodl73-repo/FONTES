# MIT Global Languages Core Custody

## Research question

What MIT Global Languages source custody should FONTES add so PROOF, FLETCH,
CROP, and PEBBLE can distinguish language-learning claims about communication,
grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation, literacy, and culture from adjacent
linguistics, NLP, literature, media, and humanities source maps?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors Global Languages custody in MIT OCW 21G.701, 21G.301,
21G.107, 21G.503, and 21G.401. MIT OCW course/listing and syllabus pages are
mapped as `derived_text_allowed`. Textbook bodies, workbook bodies, audio
programs, video episodes, LLARC materials, cassettes, CDs, CD-ROMs,
registered-student audio files, study materials, handouts, dictionaries,
assignments, compositions, quizzes, exams, projects, student performances,
images, third-party online tools, external videos, and linked third-party assets
remain boundary-checked unless separately reviewed.

| Recommendation | Owner | Validation | Non-goal |
