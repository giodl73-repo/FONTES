## Boundary notes

Textbook bodies, workbook bodies, audio programs, video episodes, LLARC
materials, cassettes, CDs, CD-ROMs, registered-student audio files, study
materials, handouts, dictionaries, assignments, compositions, quizzes, exams,
projects, student performances, images, third-party online tools, external
videos, and linked third-party assets are not cached by this tranche.
