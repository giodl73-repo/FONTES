# Climate Science, Environmental Chemistry, Sustainable Energy, and Carbon Systems Custody

## Research question

What source custody should FONTES add for climate-system science, atmospheric
chemistry, sustainable energy, and carbon-management claims before PROOF,
FLETCH, CROP, and PEBBLE make stronger claims about emissions, radiative
forcing, climate models, air chemistry, energy proposals, mitigation,
adaptation, or sequestration?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors climate, environment, and energy custody in MIT OCW 12.340,
12.340x, 1.84J, 22.081J, 12.000, and the MIT OCW Environment and Sustainability
collection. MIT OCW course/listing/syllabus/collection pages are mapped as
`derived_text_allowed`. Problem sets, exams, projects, textbooks, PDFs, lecture
videos, interactive OLL features, model files, datasets, student work, external
program pages, news links, and linked third-party materials remain
boundary-checked.

## Recommendations

| Recommendation | Owner | Validation | Non-goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adopt now | PROOF / FLETCH / CROP / PEBBLE | Separate climate observation, physical mechanism, model hierarchy, emissions pathway, chemistry process, energy technology, policy/economic goal, mitigation/adaptation proposal, and sequestration claim. | Do not treat model output, energy-system scoring, or sequestration proposal as evidence without source-backed inputs, assumptions, model version, and uncertainty boundaries. |
| Prototype behind boundary | PROOF / FLETCH | Prototype climate/energy provenance records for data source, variable, spatial/temporal scale, scenario, model family, parameterization, forcing, uncertainty, technology boundary, lifecycle/system boundary, and policy objective. | Do not cache or redistribute OCW videos, problem sets, exams, projects, textbooks, PDFs, model files, datasets, OLL interactive assets, or student work. |
| Defer | FONTES | Promote climate datasets, model code, textbook sections, project reports, external program pages, and current-news materials only after explicit reuse review. | Do not collapse physical climate science, environmental chemistry, energy engineering, and policy choice into one proof category. |
