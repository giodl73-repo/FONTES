# MIT Chemistry Core and Organic Chemistry Custody

## Research question

What MIT-major source custody should FONTES add after Physics so PROOF, FLETCH,
CROP, and PEBBLE can distinguish general chemistry, organic chemistry,
advanced/synthetic organic chemistry, chemical thermodynamics/kinetics, physical
chemistry, and inorganic chemistry claims from the existing 5.12 Organic
Chemistry I and materials/biology coverage?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors chemistry custody in MIT OCW 5.111SC, 5.13, 5.43, 5.512,
5.60, 5.61, and 5.05. MIT OCW course/listing and syllabus pages are mapped as
`derived_text_allowed`. Textbook bodies, MIT Press or commercial book bodies,
lecture notes, lecture videos, problem sets, solutions, exams, exam solutions,
recitation materials, clicker questions, behind-the-scenes videos, diagrams,
reaction schemes, molecular-structure drawings, spectroscopy data, lab data,
software/tools, student work, and linked third-party assets remain
boundary-checked unless separately reviewed.
