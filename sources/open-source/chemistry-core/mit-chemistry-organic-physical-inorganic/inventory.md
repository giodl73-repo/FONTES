## Boundary notes

Textbook bodies, MIT Press or commercial book bodies, lecture notes, lecture
videos, problem sets, solutions, exams, exam solutions, recitation materials,
clicker questions, behind-the-scenes videos, diagrams, reaction schemes,
molecular-structure drawings, spectroscopy data, lab data, software/tools,
student work, and linked third-party assets are not cached by this tranche.
