# MIT Mathematics Core II Custody

## Research question

What MIT Mathematics source custody should FONTES add after the existing
statistics/probability/algebra/topology foundations so PROOF, FLETCH, CROP, and
PEBBLE can distinguish analysis, complex variables, differential geometry,
number theory, and combinatorics claims?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors additional mathematics-major custody in MIT OCW 18.100B,
18.04, 18.950, 18.781, and 18.314. MIT OCW course/listing and syllabus pages
are mapped as `derived_text_allowed`. Textbook bodies, textbook PDFs, lecture
notes, problem-solving notes, problem sets, solutions, exams, exam solutions,
quizzes, homework, diagrams, proofs, worked examples, supplemental readings,
Google Books previews, external PDFs, and linked third-party assets remain
boundary-checked unless separately reviewed.
