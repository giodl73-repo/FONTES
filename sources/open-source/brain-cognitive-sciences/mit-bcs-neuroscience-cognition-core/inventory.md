## Boundary notes

Textbook bodies, journal articles, lecture notes, lecture videos, lecture audio,
podcasts, problem sets, homework, exams, solutions, quizzes, student papers,
experimental-design assignments, project reports, MATLAB/code files,
lab/dissection materials, images, datasets, brain talks, selected videos,
readings, and linked third-party assets are not cached by this tranche.
