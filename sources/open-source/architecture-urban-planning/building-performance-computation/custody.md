# Building Performance, Environmental Technologies, Daylighting, Construction Systems, and Design Computing Custody

## Research question

What sixth architecture and urban-planning custody should FONTES add so PROOF,
FLETCH, CROP, and PEBBLE can distinguish building-performance, daylighting,
building-energy, construction-system, and design-computing claims from general
architecture, urban design, GIS, and planning-method claims?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors sixth-wave architecture/urban-planning custody in MIT OCW
4.401 Environmental Technologies in Buildings, 4.430 Daylighting, 4.42J
Fundamentals of Energy in Buildings, 4.461 Building Technology I, and 4.500
Introduction to Design Computing. MIT OCW course/listing/syllabus pages are
mapped as `derived_text_allowed`. Lecture slides/notes, readings, textbooks,
software, plug-ins, simulation files, photographs, physical/digital models,
building evaluations, project examples, assignments, problem sets, exams,
solutions, presentations, labs, student projects, images, diagrams, datasets, and
linked third-party assets remain boundary-checked unless separately reviewed.
