# Environmental Planning, Economic Development, Land-Use Law, Urban Public Finance, and Policy Analysis Custody

## Research question

What third architecture and urban-planning custody should FONTES add so PROOF,
FLETCH, CROP, and PEBBLE can distinguish environmental planning, local economic
development, property/land-use law, urban public finance, and policy-analysis
claims from design, GIS, housing, and transportation planning claims?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors third-wave architecture/urban planning custody in MIT OCW
11.601, 11.438, 11.493, 11.487, and 11.003J. MIT OCW course/listing/syllabus
pages are mapped as `derived_text_allowed`. Readings, books, eBooks, videos,
faculty commentary, legal cases, casebooks, supplemental legal materials,
technical papers, lecture notes, assignment examples, student work, simulations,
discussion cases, problem sets, solutions, exams, presentations, papers, memos,
forum/survey/quiz data, uploaded course-site materials, community-contact data,
images, diagrams, datasets, software, and linked third-party assets remain
boundary-checked unless separately reviewed.
