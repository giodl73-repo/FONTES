## Boundary notes

Textbook bodies, MIT Press book text, Google Earth/KMZ files, ArcGIS/ESRI
software/interface material, lecture notes, image galleries, problem sets,
exams, quizzes, written assignments, papers, field-study materials,
design-project reports, existing-building assessments, GIS datasets, maps,
parcels, census extracts, employment/population forecasts, guest-speaker
materials, videos, images, diagrams, and linked third-party assets are not cached
by this tranche.
