# Architecture, Building Technology, Urban Design, Planning, and GIS Custody

## Research question

What source custody should FONTES add for architecture and urban planning so
PROOF, FLETCH, CROP, and PEBBLE can distinguish building-technology,
architectural-history, urban-design, planning-action, and GIS/spatial-analysis
claims?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors architecture and urban-planning custody in MIT OCW 4.401,
4.605, 11.001J, 11.201, and 11.520. MIT OCW course/listing/syllabus pages are
mapped as `derived_text_allowed`. Textbook bodies, MIT Press book text, Google
Earth/KMZ files, ArcGIS/ESRI software/interface material, lecture notes, image
galleries, problem sets, exams, quizzes, written assignments, papers, field-study
materials, design-project reports, existing-building assessments, GIS datasets,
maps, parcels, census extracts, employment/population forecasts, guest-speaker
materials, videos, images, diagrams, and linked third-party assets remain
boundary-checked unless separately reviewed.

## Recommendations

| Recommendation | Owner | Validation | Non-goal |
