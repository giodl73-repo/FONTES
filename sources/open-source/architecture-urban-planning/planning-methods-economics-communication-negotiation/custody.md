# Planning Methods, Economics, Communication, Research Design, and Negotiation Custody

## Research question

What fourth architecture and urban-planning custody should FONTES add so PROOF,
FLETCH, CROP, and PEBBLE can distinguish planning methods, planning economics,
research design, communication, argumentation, negotiation, and dispute-resolution
claims from substantive housing, transportation, environmental, law, finance, and
GIS claims?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors fourth-wave architecture/urban-planning custody in MIT OCW
11.220, 11.202, 11.233, 11.225, and 11.255. MIT OCW course/listing/syllabus
pages are mapped as `derived_text_allowed`. Lecture notes, readings, textbooks,
problem sets, solutions, exams, assignments, writing samples, peer-review
materials, role-play packets, negotiation scenarios, videos, taped student
exercises, instructor insights, presentations, survey/interview artifacts,
student projects, images, diagrams, datasets, software, and linked third-party
assets remain boundary-checked unless separately reviewed.
