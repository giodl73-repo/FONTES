# Transportation Systems, Urban Mobility, Transit, ITS, and Logistics Custody

## Research question

What fifth architecture and urban-planning custody should FONTES add so PROOF,
FLETCH, CROP, and PEBBLE can distinguish transportation planning, public transit,
demand/economic analysis, intelligent transportation systems, and logistics
methods from land-use/transportation interaction and general planning-method
claims?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors fifth-wave architecture/urban-planning custody in MIT OCW
1.252J, 1.258J, 1.201J, 1.212J, and 1.203J. MIT OCW course/listing/syllabus pages
are mapped as `derived_text_allowed`. Lecture notes, readings, textbooks, online
book bodies, problem sets, solutions, exams, assignments, videos, instructor
insights, walking-tour materials, public-meeting artifacts, local case data,
transit datasets, operations schedules, cost models, forecasts, project examples,
regional architecture projects, term papers, diagrams, images, software, and
linked third-party assets remain boundary-checked unless separately reviewed.
