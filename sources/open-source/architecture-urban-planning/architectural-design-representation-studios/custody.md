# Architectural Design, Representation, and Studio Custody

## Research question

What ninth architecture and urban-planning custody should FONTES add so PROOF,
FLETCH, CROP, and PEBBLE can distinguish architectural design studio,
representation, drawing/making, geometric discipline, environmental design, and
landscape-studio claims from generic building technology, design computing, or
urban design claims?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors ninth-wave architecture/urban-planning custody in MIT OCW
4.105, 4.111, 4.112, 4.123, and 4.125. MIT OCW course/listing and syllabus pages
are mapped as `derived_text_allowed`. Assignments, supporting-resource PDFs,
tutorials, project examples, student work, design products, physical/digital
models, drawings, images, lecture/demonstration videos, media assignments, text
quotations, readings, software/tool files, and linked third-party assets remain
boundary-checked unless separately reviewed.

## Recommendations

| Recommendation | Owner | Validation | Non-goal |
|---|---|---|---|
