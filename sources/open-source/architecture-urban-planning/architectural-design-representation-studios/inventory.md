## Boundary notes

Assignments, supporting-resource PDFs, tutorials, readings, quotations, lecture
or demonstration videos, media assignments, project examples, student work,
physical/digital models, drawings, images, software/tool files, site materials,
models, presentations, and linked third-party assets are not cached by this
tranche.
