# Urban Energy, Climate Adaptation, Sustainable Development, Environmental Justice, and Community Climate Action Custody

## Research question

What eighth architecture and urban-planning custody should FONTES add so PROOF,
FLETCH, CROP, and PEBBLE can distinguish urban-energy, sustainable-development,
environmental-justice, climate-adaptation, and community climate-action claims
from generic environmental planning, public finance, transportation, and building
performance claims?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors eighth-wave architecture/urban-planning custody in MIT OCW
11.165J, 11.368, 11.366J, 11.941, and RES.ENV-001. MIT OCW course/listing and
syllabus pages are mapped as `derived_text_allowed`. Readings, books/open
textbooks, lecture videos, podcasts, lecture notes, image galleries, workshop
videos, field-trip videos, assignments, reaction papers, term projects, final
papers/manuals, presentations, CoolClimate calculator outputs, methane field data,
GPS traces, handheld-probe data, maps, legal-case materials, client/community
partner materials, student work, datasets, software/tools, and linked third-party
assets remain boundary-checked unless separately reviewed.

## Recommendations

| Recommendation | Owner | Validation | Non-goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adopt now | PROOF / FLETCH / CROP / PEBBLE | Keep climate/sustainability claims distinct by system scale, justice lens, transition mechanism, municipal responsibility, community partner, data-collection method, and rights boundary. | Do not treat energy calculations, EJ findings, climate-readiness manuals, methane measurements, or community-action recommendations as reusable without source-backed scope and assumptions. |
