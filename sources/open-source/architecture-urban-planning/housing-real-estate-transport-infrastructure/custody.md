# Housing, Real Estate, Transportation, Infrastructure, and Urbanization Custody

## Research question

What second architecture and urban-planning custody should FONTES add so PROOF,
FLETCH, CROP, and PEBBLE can distinguish housing/community development, real
estate economics, transportation-land-use interaction, site/infrastructure
planning, and global urbanization/development claims?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors second-wave architecture/urban planning custody in MIT OCW
11.401, 11.433J, 11.953, 11.304J, and 11.947. MIT OCW course/listing/syllabus
pages are mapped as `derived_text_allowed`. Readings, books, journal articles,
lecture notes, lecture videos, problem sets, assignments, written examples,
exams, exam solutions, team projects, forum posts, computer-based exercises,
GIS/model outputs, maps, datasets, site inventories, landscape/physiography
maps, client/project materials, studio outputs, handouts, presentations, papers,
case-study materials, images, diagrams, and linked third-party assets remain
boundary-checked unless separately reviewed.

## Recommendations

| Recommendation | Owner | Validation | Non-goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adopt now | PROOF / FLETCH / CROP / PEBBLE | Keep housing equity, real-estate market, transport-land-use, site infrastructure, and urbanization/development claims distinct by scale, place, market segment, policy instrument, dataset, design artifact, client/studio context, and rights boundary. | Do not treat a policy claim, market forecast, map, studio output, case interpretation, or planning memo as a reusable fact without source-backed assumptions. |

| Defer | FONTES | Promote concrete datasets, maps, market forecasts, site plans, project reports, studio work, policy memos, or paper arguments only after explicit reuse review. | Do not collapse housing, real estate, transport planning, infrastructure planning, and global urbanization into one generic urban-planning proof category. |
