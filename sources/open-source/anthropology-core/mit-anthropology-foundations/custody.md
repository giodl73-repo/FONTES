# MIT Anthropology Core Custody

## Research question

What MIT Anthropology source custody should FONTES add so PROOF, FLETCH, CROP,
and PEBBLE can distinguish cultural-anthropology, symbolism, race/science,
power, and cyberculture claims from the existing broad social-cultural systems
source map?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors MIT Anthropology custody in MIT OCW 21A.00, 21A.212,
21A.240, 21A.245J, and 21A.850J. MIT OCW course/listing and syllabus pages are
mapped as `derived_text_allowed`. Lecture notes, required readings,
supplementary readings, book bodies, article/chapter bodies, written
assignments, papers, research papers, commentary papers, field reports, final
projects, exams, problem sets, films, games, discussion artifacts, class-web-site
materials, student work, reference databases, images, PDFs, and linked
third-party assets remain boundary-checked unless separately reviewed.
