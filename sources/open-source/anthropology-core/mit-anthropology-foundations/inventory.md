## Boundary notes

Lecture notes, required readings, supplementary readings, book bodies,
article/chapter bodies, written assignments, papers, research papers, commentary
papers, field reports, final projects, exams, problem sets, films, games,
discussion artifacts, class-web-site materials, student work, reference
databases, images, PDFs, and linked third-party assets are not cached by this
tranche.
