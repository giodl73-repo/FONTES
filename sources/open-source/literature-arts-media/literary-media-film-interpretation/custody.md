# Literature, Arts, Media Studies, Film, and Interpretation Custody

## Research question

What source custody should FONTES add for literature, arts, media studies, and
film before PROOF, FLETCH, CROP, and PEBBLE make stronger claims about
interpretation, literary theory, poetry, fiction, media influence, cinematic
analysis, adaptation, narrative, cultural artifacts, or communication?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors literature/arts/media custody in MIT OCW 21L.015,
21L.011, 21L.706, and Open Yale ENGL 300, ENGL 310, and ENGL 291. MIT OCW and
Open Yale course/listing/syllabus pages are mapped as `derived_text_allowed`.
Textbook bodies, anthologies, course packets, poems, novels, literary works,
film clips, films, television episodes, screenings, lecture videos, audio,
transcripts, slides, archival materials, student papers, exams, images, media
assets, publisher pages, and linked third-party assets remain boundary-checked
unless separately reviewed.
