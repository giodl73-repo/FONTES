## Boundary notes

Assigned books, articles, films, videos, media works, images, interviews,
transcripts, student writing, workshop uploads, drafts, portfolios, examples,
lecture notes, exams, handouts, textbook bodies, and linked third-party assets
are not cached by this tranche.
