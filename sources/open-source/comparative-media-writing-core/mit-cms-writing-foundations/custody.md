# MIT Comparative Media Studies/Writing Core Custody

## Research question

What MIT Comparative Media Studies/Writing source custody should FONTES add so
PROOF, FLETCH, CROP, and PEBBLE can distinguish media-study, rhetoric,
science-writing, and creative-writing claims from existing broad literature,
media, game-design, and humanities source maps?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors CMS/Writing custody in MIT OCW CMS.100, CMS.796, 21W.011,
21W.035, and 21W.755. MIT OCW course/listing and syllabus pages are mapped as
`derived_text_allowed`. Assigned books, articles, films, videos, media works,
images, interviews, transcripts, student writing, workshop uploads, drafts,
portfolios, examples, lecture notes, exams, handouts, textbook bodies, and
linked third-party assets remain boundary-checked unless separately reviewed.

## Recommendations

| Recommendation | Owner | Validation | Non-goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adopt now | PROOF / FLETCH / CROP / PEBBLE | Keep claims distinct as media studies, comparative media texts, rhetoric/civic writing, science writing, and short fiction craft. | Do not duplicate existing broad literature/media/humanities or game-design source maps. |
| Prototype behind boundary | PROOF / FLETCH | Prototype evidence nodes for media analysis, adaptation, rhetorical situation, civic argument, public science communication, interview practice, short-story craft, revision, and workshop process. | Do not cache or redistribute assigned books, articles, films, videos, images, student writing, drafts, interviews, transcripts, portfolios, or third-party media. |
