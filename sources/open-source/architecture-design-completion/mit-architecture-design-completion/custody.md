# MIT Architecture Design Completion Custody

## Research question

What MIT Architecture design source custody should FONTES add to complete the
department/core map without duplicating prior urban-planning, building
technology, or architecture-history rows?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors final architecture-design custody in MIT OCW 4.111, 4.112,
and 4.125. MIT OCW course/listing and syllabus pages are mapped as
`derived_text_allowed`. Assignments, drawings, models, portfolios, project
files, studio documentation, images, site data, critique materials,
presentations, readings, PDFs, student work, and linked third-party assets
remain boundary-checked unless separately reviewed.
