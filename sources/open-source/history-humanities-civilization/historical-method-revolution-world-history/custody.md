# History, Humanities, Civilization, Revolution, and Historical Method Custody

## Research question

What source custody should FONTES add for history and humanities before PROOF,
FLETCH, CROP, and PEBBLE make stronger claims about civilization, revolution,
war, empire, historical method, primary/secondary sources, social change,
cultural development, or historical causation?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors history/humanities custody in MIT OCW 21H.001 and
21H.009 plus Open Yale/Yale Online HIST 202, HIST 116, and HIST 119. MIT OCW
and Yale course/listing/syllabus pages are mapped as `derived_text_allowed`.
Textbook bodies, readings, primary-source editions, films, art reproductions,
music, lecture notes, lecture videos, audio, transcripts, exams, student
papers, discussion/forum posts, museum materials, images, and linked
third-party assets remain boundary-checked unless separately reviewed.

## Recommendations

| Recommendation | Owner | Validation | Non-goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adopt now | PROOF / FLETCH / CROP / PEBBLE | Separate event, period, actor, institution, primary source, secondary interpretation, method, geography, chronology, causal claim, and memory/cultural representation. | Do not treat a course topic, lecture title, or textbook assignment as a historical fact without source-backed context and evidence type. |
| Prototype behind boundary | PROOF / FLETCH | Prototype provenance for historical causation, revolution comparison, empire/nation-state claims, war/reconstruction claims, and primary-vs-secondary evidence use. | Do not cache or redistribute textbooks, readings, films, art/music assets, lecture notes, transcripts, student papers, exams, forum posts, museum materials, or third-party linked assets. |
| Defer | FONTES | Promote specific primary-source editions, art/film/music materials, lecture transcripts, or textbook sections only after explicit reuse review. | Do not collapse history, humanities interpretation, political theory, memory, and social-science explanation into one proof category. |
