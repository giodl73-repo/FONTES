## Boundary notes

Lecture notes, videos, problem-solving videos, problem sets, solutions, quizzes,
exams, supplemental exam materials, open-textbook bodies, commercial textbook
bodies, journal articles, readings, book chapters, films, written assignments,
grading rubrics, datasets, Stata/SAS logs, statistical-software artifacts, MITx
platform/certificate materials, instructor insights, images, PDFs, and linked
third-party assets are not cached by this tranche.
