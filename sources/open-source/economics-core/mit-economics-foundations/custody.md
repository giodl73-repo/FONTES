# MIT Economics Core Custody

## Research question

What MIT Economics source custody should FONTES add so PROOF, FLETCH, CROP,
and PEBBLE can distinguish microeconomics, macroeconomics, statistical methods,
econometrics, and development-economics claims from the existing broad
economics-market-systems source map?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors MIT Economics custody in MIT OCW 14.01SC, 14.02, 14.30,
14.32, and 14.73. MIT OCW course/listing and syllabus pages are mapped as
`derived_text_allowed`. Lecture notes, videos, problem-solving videos, problem
sets, solutions, quizzes, exams, supplemental exam materials, open-textbook
bodies, commercial textbook bodies, journal articles, readings, book chapters,
films, written assignments, grading rubrics, datasets, Stata/SAS logs,
statistical-software artifacts, MITx platform/certificate materials, instructor
insights, images, PDFs, and linked third-party assets remain boundary-checked
unless separately reviewed.

## Recommendations

| Recommendation | Owner | Validation | Non-goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adopt now | PROOF / FLETCH / CROP / PEBBLE | Keep claims distinct as microeconomics, macroeconomics, statistical methods, econometrics, and development economics. | Do not duplicate the broad economics-market-systems source map. |
| Prototype behind boundary | PROOF / FLETCH | Prototype evidence nodes for supply/demand, macro policy, economic statistics, regression/program evaluation, and poverty/development interventions. | Do not cache or redistribute notes, videos, textbooks, journal articles, datasets, software logs, exams, problem sets, written assignments, films, PDFs, or third-party material. |
| Defer | FONTES | Promote specific readings, datasets, videos, MITx assets, or empirical software artifacts only after source-scope and rights review. | Do not infer reuse rights from OCW listing/syllabus text alone. |
