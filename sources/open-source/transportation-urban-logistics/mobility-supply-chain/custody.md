# Transportation Systems, Urban Mobility, Logistics, Supply Chain, and Infrastructure Planning Custody

## Research question

What source custody should FONTES add for transportation systems, urban
mobility, logistics, supply chains, and infrastructure planning before PROOF,
FLETCH, CROP, and PEBBLE make stronger claims about travel demand, congestion,
routing, fleet operations, freight, inventory, supply-chain risk, infrastructure
finance, project evaluation, or mobility policy?

## Current custody decision

This tranche anchors transportation, urban mobility, logistics, supply-chain,
and infrastructure-planning custody in MIT OCW 1.201J, 1.221J, 1.252J, ESD.260J,
15.762J, 1.040, and the MIT OCW Transportation collection. MIT OCW
course/listing/syllabus/collection pages are mapped as `derived_text_allowed`.
Problem sets, exams, readings, textbooks, case studies, team projects,
spreadsheets, real-data files, walking-tour materials, lecture notes, examples,
PDFs, images, and linked third-party materials remain boundary-checked.
