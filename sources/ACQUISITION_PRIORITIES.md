# FONTES Acquisition Priorities

FONTES should grow by source-custody value, not by mirroring everything that is
online. The first expansion stays close to the portfolio's strongest needs:
computing, AI, mathematics, physics for object/world behavior, and chemistry for
real items and materials.

## Priority order

| Rank | Domain | Why it comes here | First source families |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Computer science and software | Directly supports MAXIM, AI Methodology, tools-infra, and coding-agent context packs. | MIT OCW, Stanford Engineering Everywhere, Open Michigan OER. |
| 2 | AI and machine learning | Anchors model, agent, optimization, and knowledge-system reasoning without relying on ad hoc web snippets. | MIT OCW 6.034/6.036, Stanford SEE CS229, MIT Press/author-hosted books. |
| 3 | Mathematics | Supplies the shared language for algorithms, optimization, simulation, graphics, physics, and evidence scoring. | MIT 18.06, Stanford EE263/EE364A, MIT 18.065, Open Yale where available. |
| 4 | Physics for objects | Grounds games/design systems in motion, forces, energy, collision, optics, waves, fluids, and material behavior. | MIT 8.01/8.02, Open Yale PHYS 200/201, MIT mechanics/physics OCW. |
| 5 | Chemistry for real items | Grounds materials, pigments, fuels, ceramics, food, medicine, corrosion, toxicity, reactions, and craft plausibility. | MIT 5.111/5.12, Open Yale CHEM 125a/b, Open Michigan chemistry OER. |
| 6 | Game-design support domains | Supplements games-design repos after the hard-science spine is in place. | Psychology, economics/game theory, ecology, architecture, music, art, narrative, anthropology. |

## Game-design bridge

After the first five domains are source-mapped, FONTES should add targeted
source families for games-design:

| Game-design need | Source domains |
|---|---|
| Object behavior | mechanics, collisions, materials, optics, acoustics. |
| Real items | chemistry, materials science, food science, toxicology, metallurgy, ceramics, textiles. |
| Worlds and hazards | ecology, weather, geology, hydrology, fire science. |
| Players and puzzles | cognition, psychology, game theory, education, linguistics. |
| Culture and story | anthropology, history, ritual, music, art, architecture. |

## Guardrail

Do not start with broad "all courses" ingestion. Each target enters through:

1. source-family terms;
2. course/work record;
3. fetch policy;
4. FLETCH registry;
5. PROOF custody record;
6. PEBBLE/CROP outputs only when text transfer is allowed.
